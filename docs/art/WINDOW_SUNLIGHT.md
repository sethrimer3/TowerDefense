# Window light and the Tiles stained glass

![The Tiles window and soft sunlight at phone scale](tiles-sunlight.png)

The Library and Tiles hall share `src/soft-sunlight.ts`. Each renderer caches three small light-density textures once. Their cross-section has a Gaussian feather, a smooth source fade and a gradual distance fade. Each frame shears these fields with the solar heading and composites them with `screen`. The fields overlap at low opacity; their edges dissolve instead of outlining transparent polygons. Only light is interpolated: stone, oak, lead and the rest of the scene keep their existing crisp sampling. There is no full-scene blur or per-frame texture allocation.

Tiles follows the Library's eight-minute day, with its own slower, wider sway (`hallSway`, `hallSwayMs`) and the Library's few-second pulse on each of its five shafts, drawn narrower than their spacing so each stands apart. The shafts and dust are drawn every frame onto their own layer, `.chamber-light`, which sits over the whole room, the menu included, and is composited with CSS `mix-blend-mode: screen`: the shelf and its tiles are lit where a shaft crosses them rather than hiding it. Its seventy dust motes follow stable wall-clock paths, drifting up or down and wandering, and glint only inside the light, fading at both ends; they use no gameplay/effects random stream, new timer or save state. Night clears the layer. Reduce Motion freezes the Tiles solar heading and omits its drifting dust. The keep's dim veil and shelf backing are lighter than the other rooms' so the window and light show through. The Library retains its existing effects/Reduce Motion behavior; its motes (`DUST` in `library/render.ts`) come in unseen at a beam's edge and ease their opacity toward the beam's strength where they float (`shaftAt` samples the same field the shafts are drawn from), brightening quickly and dimming slowly, so one drifting out fades away and one drifting back brightens again; a mote is removed only once it has faded to nothing.

With Pixel Art checked, the Tiles window is glazed pane by pane (`paneAt`): a border of alternating cream and blue beads, a rose in the head (cream eye, eight blue petals, clear crescents between them, an outer ring of sixteen segments each holding a cream roundel, eight in a small window), faintly blue spandrels with cream roundels, and two pointed lancets of diamond quarries either side of a mullion. `leadedPane` turns pane boundaries into one-pixel cames (lead on the pixel toward the mullion or above, so the window mirrors exactly), and shades each pane on its own, lighter along its top came. The glass is pale clear, blue and cream mixed over the city outside (about half at noon). As an easter egg the rose isn't always the flower: `roseOn` rolls once a day, at midnight (a quarter of `DAY_MS` before the day's turn, while the glass is dark), and about half the days it is set with another of `ROSES` (`ROSE_PANES`): diamond quarries filling the disc, a winged sword pointing down, the sun with its rays between two crescent moons and the stars, an abstract of cells round mirrored seeds, or two hexagrams of interlocking triangles tinted by how many overlap. These alone add a few warmer gold panes (`GOLD`): the sword's hilt and alternate feathers, the sun and alternate rays and stars, the deepest triangle overlaps, a few cells and quarries. Each reads the folded `|x|`, so it mirrors exactly like the rest. `tilesRose("sword")` in the console pins one; `tilesRose()` hands it back to the nights. Switching Pixel Art off rebakes the original four-lobed clear-glass window; toggling back works at the same size without reloading. This revises the existing procedural backdrop; it adds no sprite assets.

Tuning:

| Control | Location |
| --- | --- |
| Light intensity | `SUNLIGHT.density` (Library) and `hallDensity` (Tiles) in `src/soft-sunlight.ts`; callers also pass daylight/pulse strength |
| Edge softness and widening | `SUNLIGHT.feather` and `spread` |
| Fade with distance | `SUNLIGHT.distanceFade` (Library), `hallFade` (Tiles); source fade in the texture bake |
| Motion | `SUNLIGHT.sway` / `swayMs`, plus `hallSway` / `hallSwayMs` in Tiles; solar period remains `DAY_MS` |
| Dust density and speed | `SUNLIGHT.dustCount`; trajectory periods in `HallBackdrop.drawLight` |
| Pane colors and transmission | `GLASS_TINTS` and the tint mix in `HallBackdrop.draw` |
| Tracery and pane layout | `paneAt` / `leadedPane` in `src/ui/hall-art.ts`; rose glazings in `ROSE_PANES`, their odds in `roseOn` |

No runtime blur or layer-count control is needed: the cached continuous density replaces nested polygons. Five fields are drawn in each, with three reusable 96 × 192 textures per renderer.

`node tests/hall-sunlight.mjs` checks soft edge falloff, plainly visible shafts, changing sunlight/dust, night, Reduce Motion, native symmetry and repeated art toggles at 1280/390/360 pixels. It captures both the real Tiles UI and an unobstructed window under `test-results/tiles-sunlight-*` and `tiles-window-detail-*`. Run against a dev server with `TEST_URL` and `PLAYWRIGHT_EXECUTABLE` as needed. Existing `tests/library-idle.mjs` and `tests/tiles-marks.mjs` cover live Library rendering and Tiles interactions.
