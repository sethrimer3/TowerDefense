# Repair shop

Implemented from the 2026-10-31 request. Unspecified tuning below is an initial assumption.

- **Building:** Repair shop (`repairShop`), City category, bought in Tiles for 3 Copper at the existing copy-price curve. A 2 × 1 quarter-grid footprint (4 × 2 simulation cells), 160 HP, placed inside the city. No free copy is added to old saves.
- **Initial skill:** Carpentry is an owned, free tree node from the beginning. It rebuilds houses and service buildings, including fallen Repair shops. Monster bait still needs its existing Restocking allowance.
- **Knowledge tree:** independent branches shared by all Repair shops, with normal `studyPathCost(2, rank)` costs, immediate purchases, free undo and respec. Advanced carpentry (one rank) repairs damaged or destroyed infantry buildings and towers, plus a damaged keep. Masonry (one rank) repairs damaged or destroyed walls, gates and wall ballistas. Workshop crew has two ranks: 2 → 3 → 4 workers per shop. Critical repairs (one rank) prioritizes the keep, breaches, Repair shops and disabled/critically damaged defenses over houses; travelling workers reassess every second. Parting shot (one rank) unlocks a weak arrow while fleeing: base 2 damage, 2.5-second cooldown.
- **Recruitment:** only intact, owned, visible Repair shops recruit. Each slot belongs to its shop, workers emerge at its door, return indoors between jobs, and a killed slot waits 10 seconds before replacement. Existing workers can finish repairs and restore their fallen shop. No automatic builders emerge from houses.
- **Repair work:** one worker reserves a building, stands beside it, and restores 100% maximum HP in 30 seconds of active work regardless of HP or footprint. Destroyed structures return cell by cell and function only when complete. Standing structures regain HP continuously. Ice, ownership, flight and bait restock rules still apply. Workers avoid unsafe/unreachable jobs and retreat from enemies within 3 cells; bow shots happen while retreating and native projectile reflection returns to the worker. Sand cleanup is a lower-priority job.
- **Smithy:** Repair speed (`rebuild`) adds 4% work rate per rank, 30 ranks. Repair worker HP (`repairHp`) and bow damage (`repairDamage`) each add 5% per rank, 50 ranks. Building HP (`buildingHp`) and Tower HP (`towerHp`) add 5% per rank, 50 ranks. Existing timed metal costs apply. Repair worker rows are filed in City → Repair shop; Tower HP in Realm → All towers. Building HP affects troop and service structures; keep/walls retain their own HP rows.
- **Existing Forge investments:** `civilianHealth` still raises base worker HP; `rebuildSpeed` still accelerates work. `civilianCount` becomes Spare tools (+10% repair rate per level), preserving its levels without bypassing the new crew skills.
- **Old passive repair upgrades:** Stonework III now doubles placed-wall worker repair rate. Ironbound II raises gate worker repair rate by 50%; Ironbound III rebuilds fallen gates 150% faster. Neither heals nor resurrects a structure automatically.
- **State:** optional `defend.repairRanks`, validated against the tree; snapshots into `Bonuses.repair`, including native Siege castles. New training ranks decode to zero in old saves. Active workers remain run-local.

Validation: `tests/defend-repair-shop.test.ts`, updated builder/restocking/sand/wall/gate tests, save and replay goldens for the intended recruitment change, desktop/phone tree purchases and refunds, full suite and build.
