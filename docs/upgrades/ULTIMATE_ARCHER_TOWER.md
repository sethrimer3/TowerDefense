# Ultimate Archer Tower (`chronoTower`)

Requested: an idle-friendly target and time-stopped arsenal, a 45° rapid release, independent storage/Divine Arrow/focus/amplification branches.

- **Panel:** per-building Knowledge tree in Defend. Merge three Archer Towers maxed on Fire arrows, Focused fire and Wisp Arrow, like the other ultimates; no extra merge fee. Independent shared branches use the existing side-rank mechanism, including undo/respec, saves and battle snapshots.
- **Assumptions/tuning:** 30 physical shots initially, storage upgrades to 60/90 (6,006/24,006 Knowledge). Field/trigger range follows the ordinary Archer Tower range; 2×2 footprint, 350 HP. Only idle attackers in their ordinary range contribute; no friendly damage or pathing toward the tower. Continuous minelaying already has work to do and does not generate extra shots; idle Wisp towers can contribute Divine damage at their normal archer reload rate. Harmless nanobot payloads have no direct damage to contribute. Physical arrows/bullets/shells/ballista bolts are compatible; magic, beams and melee feed Divine Arrow after unlock. Released shots cannot be recaptured by any Ultimate Archer Tower. If the field fills while an idle cannon shell is in flight, that support shell expires harmlessly instead of exploding on allies.
- **Release:** enemy in range triggers the stored physical arsenal, one shot every 0.04s, aiming a fixed 45° fan at the trigger position. Arrows and bolts fly straight; shell arcs aim at the trigger distance along their fan direction. Retained explosive/burning/piercing payloads keep their existing combat rules. Focus costs 1/10 Knowledge, narrowing the total fan to 20°/0°. A committed volley completes even if its original target dies.
- **Divine Arrow:** independent unlock and four upgrades, holding 50/100/150/200/250 incompatible hits. Costs 25/25,025/100,025/225,025/400,025 Knowledge. Each hit stores its incoming damage once; maxed reserves reject further idle contributions. When an enemy is in range, launch the reserve as one fast straight arrow to the map edge, sweeping every enemy along its ray once, including flying targets. Reset hit count and stored damage at launch. Gold beam trail and dust are decorative, with Reduce Motion variants.
- **Divine amplification:** independent 2×/5×/10× total damage multipliers, costing 100,000/1,000,000/10,000,000 Knowledge. Requires Divine Arrow to have an effect; buying the branch independently is allowed. Do not compound multipliers or apply source attack bonuses twice.
- **Lifetime:** each tower owns its transient arsenal and Divine reserve. No ammunition persists across runs/reload. Destruction discards the reserve. Exclude friendly towers from enemy lists, wave counts, rewards, enemy AI and enemy buffs.
- **Implementation:** catalog/lineage/decoding/art registration, shared side tree and stats, bounded simulation storage and projectile interception, idle attack fallbacks, procedural field/orbits/Divine trail rendering; no external assets or animation loop.

Costs and unspecified tuning are implementation assumptions and can be revised.
