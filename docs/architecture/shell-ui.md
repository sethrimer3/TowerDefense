# Shell, UI and saves

Startup, the app shell, settings, localization, rooms/tiles, sound, profile, tab icons, viewport, saves and randomness.

Moved from `AGENTS.md`; keep it current with the code it describes.

**Startup.** `index.html` contains the loading screen and an inert, hidden app. `src/boot.ts` loads CSS and the lightweight `ui/loading-screen.ts` renderer before dynamically importing `main.ts`. The loading wall reuses `HallBackdrop`/`paintSconce`, and the falling progress bricks share exact geometry and paint with `KeepBricks` through `ui/brick-wall.ts` (no simulation RNG). `daylight.ts` owns the shared day clock, re-exported from `library/render.ts` for existing callers. The last brick waits for initialization and fonts; boot then uncovers the app and calls `startGame()` to open native profile/welcome dialogs and start the game loop. Saved and system Reduce Motion freeze the torch/dots and skip the fall. Nothing may hold the wall forever (`ui/boot-guard.ts`): fonts are awaited at most 3 s (`within`), the first painted frame at most 200 ms, a paint error is logged and the bar's clock still runs, and `complete` uncovers the game on a timer if no animation frame comes. A start still loading after 10 s shows Try again (`slow`) while it keeps waiting; a failed import or a start stalled 25 s reloads once by itself (`mayAutoReload`, at most once a minute through sessionStorage), else shows the reload button. `tests/boot-guard.test.ts`; `node tests/loading-screen.mjs` checks desktop/phone/landscape, animation, reduced motion, startup, retry, a stalled import, a paint error and stalled fonts (dev server required).

**Shell.** `main.ts` builds the shell (`ui/shell.ts`: the currency bar of the Mine's Copper, Silver and Gold (`save.smithy`), Knowledge, upgrade points and Blight Essence (`save.essence`, "Essence", optional in saves: Defend's Dark One kills earn it, nothing spends it yet. `defend/essence.ts`'s `essenceFor` gives each kill 1 to 3 (more for bigger Blight, fortresses more; a small worm about 10 in all, a medium about 30, a boss worm's segments its `bossLuck`, 1 to 20 each, 50 to 1000 a worm), hashed from the run's seed, the enemy's id and kind, never `sim.rand`; `DefendSim.essence` counts it outside the replayed state, a boss worm's pieces finding their head through `wormRoot`, and `DefendPage.payEssence` pays it through the host's `earnEssence` at most once a second, never for the Unit tester; Ambush and Siege pay none; `tests/defend-essence.test.ts`), all on one line at any width, a wooden sign shown only on the Mine, Library and Tiles tabs (`signTab`, `.show-currencies` on the shell: elsewhere lifted out of sight and taking no room, arriving it drops in and swings to a stop, `sign-drop`, cut under Reduce Motion), its icons pixel art drawn in code (`ui/currency-icons.ts`: `iconPixels` ingots counting up for colour blindness (`bar`, `pile`, `INGOTS`: Copper one bar, Silver two stacked, Gold three in a triangle, the outline's ink parting them), an open book and an arrow out of a gem, outline-free art ringed afterwards by `outlined`; `essencePixels` a black glob whose edge is Perlin noise read round a circle through time, ringed in fire, stepped about ten times a second by `EssenceGlob` from the frame loop, still under Reduce Motion; the hemp ropes `ropePixels`/`knotPixels` as `--rope-art`/`--rope-knot`; `tests/currency-icons.test.ts`), counts from 10,000 shortened to 12.4k, 1.2M (`compact` in `main.ts`, the exact count in the tooltip), the pages and the tab row: Defend, Mine, Library, Tiles, Settings; the Mine's and the Library's pages are `descentHtml` skeletons, the tab's scene over the earth and the room below it), loads the save, owns the current tab and `navigate()`, runs the requestAnimationFrame loop (the Mine's and the Library's work on every tab, Defend's and the Mine's frames (a scene only while it is in view), the rooms below the Mine and the Library and the Tiles tab's hall, and a once-a-second tick that settles Training and Forge against the mine's smiths, and Study against the Library's researchers), and passes pages an `AppContext` (`ui/app.ts`). It also creates the `DefendPage` with its `DefendHost`: the Defend save slice, `bonuses()`, and `openTiles` (the header's Tiles button, where the Armory used to be), and `earnWave`, which awards upgrade points through `progression.ts`. The Settings page (`ui/settings-page.ts`, rows in `SETTINGS`; its page scrolls with no visible scrollbar, and each major section (Gameplay, Display and sound, Dev options) folds shut under its heading (`section`, `bindSections`: a grid row easing between 0fr and 1fr, the folded body `inert`, kept shut through redraws for the session, not saved; `node tests/settings-sections.mjs`)) opens with a Gameplay group (Show items you have none of, `showEmpty`: off by default, so the Defend palette lists only items with copies left, the host's `showEmpty`; Carry dragged items above the finger, `raiseDrag`, on by default for touch screens; Triple tap to place the rally flag, `tripleTapFlag`) and shows the Tile grid lines toggle's opacity slider only while it is on. Its dev options sit under ALL ON: Unlimited money (`devMode`: ∞ everywhere, every purchase free), All towers unlocked (`devTowers`: `unlockAllTowers` gives one of every structure when turned on), All journal entries unlocked (`devJournal`: while on, the host's `allJournal` makes the enemy journal list every species, `allEnemyKinds` in `journal.ts`; `save.defend.discovered` and the unread seal are untouched), Instantaneous research (`instantResearch`: any Study project completes at once, researcher or none; Smithy work still takes smiths and time) and All research unlocked, one per tree (`devSmithy`, `devCommand`, `devStewardship`, `devMine`, `devLibrary`, `DEV_RESEARCH`: while on, `skillRank`/`trainingRank` count every rank of it as bought; the save's ranks are untouched). Add 1 hour of idle time (`addIdle`) gives the Mine (`MinePage.addAway`: tick-by-tick catch-up, up to 24 hours banked) and the Library (`LibraryPage.addAway`: ordinary simulation steps spending the same idle-time bank) that time, moves all active project clocks back, and shows the welcome-back screen with what it made. Unlock 100 more waves (`unlockWaves` in `defend/progress.ts`) raises the Defend save's `unlockedWave` to 100 past the furthest of it and the best wave. `defendDebug(seconds, weather?)` in the console fast-forwards a battle, `mineDebug(minutes, hire?, weather?)` the mine (forcing the weather), `mineLevel(id, level?)` sets a mine building's level (or, with none, upgrades it for free, rebuild and all), `mineLook(building, zoom?)` zooms in on a mine building and picks it, `libraryDebug(shelves, librarians, seconds, fire?)` furnishes and runs the library (and sets a table alight), `libraryLab(level?)` raises the alchemy lab for free and fills it with researchers, and `libraryEnchant(n?)` enchants that many shelved books, and `tilesRose(name?)` pins the Tiles window's rose glazing (none for the night's own). While the Mine and the Library spend idle time, a golden countdown (`.tab-idle`, `idleTimers` in `main.ts`) sits at the top of their tab buttons, hidden on the tab showing; the two spend it together, neither more than `IDLE_LEAD` (`src/away.ts`) ahead of the other (each `advance` takes the other's `owedMs`), and both buttons show the longer.

**Welcome back.** `src/away.ts` holds `MAX_AWAY_MS` (24 hours, the cap on paid time away) and `span` (time in words). `ui/welcome.ts` (`WelcomeBack`) shows, on opening the game after `WELCOME_MS` or more away with something earned, the shared parchment dialog with the time away, the Library's Knowledge and the Mine's Smithy points by metal on nailed oak boards, refreshed each frame while the Mine still catches up (and whether the library burnt down while away, and what it lost); `main.ts` measures the time away from the Mine's and the Library's `savedAt` before loading them. The rewards are paid whether or not it shows.

**Player profile.** Blank name submissions generate a four-digit `player#____` name with Web Crypto; `#` is accepted by validation and save decoding. Optional `Save.profile` contains a validated 2–24-character name and a 16×24 flag (palette base plus up to 12 pattern/color layers with optional 25/50/75/100% opacity (absent means 100% for old flags)). `profile/model.ts` owns pure masks, bounded decoding, bottom-to-top RGB alpha compositing and matching canvas/SVG output; no images or arbitrary markup enter saves. `profile/editor.ts` owns a dedicated native dialog with palette, stamp previews, layer ordering/recolor/removal and bounded undo history. Missing/invalid profiles (including old saves) trigger mandatory setup before the welcome-back dialog; game progress is preserved. Profile editing in Siege pauses a running raid and uses a draft; Cancel/Escape discard it. Erase Everything prompts for a new profile. Siege displays the escaped username and the same pixel flag beside MMR. `tests/profile.test.ts` checks names, save decoding and compositing; `node tests/profile.mjs` exercises first entry, layering/undo, edit/cancel, Siege display and old saves on desktop/phone. Main-app browser tests must complete setup or seed a valid profile in their isolated save.

**Localization.** `src/i18n/` owns nine local catalogs, stable language codes, typed `t()` messages, interpolation and English fallback. `settings.language` defaults/migrates to English; Settings opens a native dialog with three columns of native-name buttons. `ctx.changeLanguage()` saves and updates annotated labels without reloading or rebuilding canvas pages. Dynamic Mine/Library text cache keys include the locale. Keep IDs/names/gameplay data unlocalized; use explicit messages and `data-i18n` on text-only labels, never automatic DOM text replacement. This first pass covers Settings, navigation and primary controls; longer descriptions remain English. Alembic includes the Latin catalogs’ accented glyphs; CJK uses local system fonts through `--font`, retaining the saved custom-font preference. `tests/i18n.test.ts` and `node tests/languages.mjs` check migration/catalogs, real desktop/phone picker, live battles, reload and menu fit. [Guide](docs/LOCALIZATION.md).

**Repeated purchases.** `src/ui/hold-repeat.ts` delegates pointer holds by stable purchase attributes so buttons can redraw between purchases. Mine hires/building upgrades, Library shelves/hires/lab expansion, Tiles purchases and repeatable Smithy/Study upgrades share it. The first held purchase waits 450 ms, then gaps shrink from 260 ms by 0.86 per purchase down to 35 ms. Short clicks and keyboard activation remain single purchases; held releases add none. Each purchase uses its normal current price and availability checks. Disabled, removed, hidden or inert controls, pointer movement/cancellation, scrolling, blur and page hiding stop the hold. Timed work and rebuilding retain their existing locks. `tests/hold-repeat.html` is an isolated redraw fixture; `node tests/hold-repeat.mjs` also tests real Mine/Library mouse/touch purchases in fresh browser contexts without player saves.

**Scroll arrows.** `ui/scroll-arrows.ts` (`installScrollArrows`, from main; `SCROLLERS`: Settings, the Defend palette (buildings, battle items, the Unit tester's enemies), Ambush's sidebar shelf and Siege's squad row) hides those menus' scrollbars and shows a pixel brass arrow (`--pixel-arrow` in `theme.css`, shared with the Settings sections' fold arrows) at the top middle while more is above and the bottom middle while more is below (left/right middle for the sideways squad row). Arrows are absolutely placed siblings of their menu, rechecked on scroll, resize (menu and parent), content changes and transitions; pointer-events none. Presentation only; `node tests/settings-sections.mjs`.

**Visible browser area.** `ui/viewport.ts` (`fitViewport`, installed once by main) sizes the shell with `--app-height` from the unzoomed VisualViewport, falling back to innerHeight and CSS dynamic/legacy viewport units. Resize/pageshow/visual viewport resize/scroll events share one queued animation frame; there is no continuous loop. Keyboard viewport offsets use `--app-offset-top`; pinch zoom and transient zero heights leave the logical layout intact. The shell has no fixed minimum height, clips its own contents, and reserves `--safe-top/right/bottom/left` once (CSS safe-area env values); navigation adds only its normal padding. Pages scroll internally as needed. Run `node tests/phone-viewport.mjs` for all tabs and Study across phone/desktop sizes, rotation, reported browser/keyboard occlusion, cutouts, pinch zoom and no-VisualViewport fallback. Screenshots are in ignored test-results.

**Landscape layout.** `ui/landscape.ts` (`watchLandscape`, installed by `boot.ts` before anything lays out) sets `:root.landscape` while the window is wider than tall (`min-aspect-ratio: 11/10`): a desktop window or a phone turned sideways. A square or tall window, a narrow desktop window included, keeps the portrait layout (Seth's choice). Nothing is rotated. On a simulation page (Defend, an Ambush or Siege battle, the Mine, the Library) the page fills the whole window, its frame just inside every edge, and the tabs (a column on the left), the currency sign, the controls along the top and the palette or Ambush bar along the bottom float over it with no panels behind them. The palette scrolls sideways (`touch-action: pan-x`). While building on Defend, Build and Tiles (`#defend-left`) stand stacked in the bottom-left corner (fixed, since the page fills the window) with the palette beside them, and Start Defense stays centred along the top. Other pages keep a grid with the tab column on the left. Every battle board (landscape or portrait) zooms out only until the square field exactly fits its canvas along the tighter side (`DefendRenderer.resize` fits to the canvas itself, beside the Defend side panel too): a wide window shows the area's wilds (`defend/wilds.ts`) left and right but stops at the field's top and bottom, and a phone shows them above and below but never past the field's sides. A building menu's framing may overscroll past the edge; `settle` returns to it.

**Rooms and tiles.** `ui/chamber.ts`: a `Chamber` is a menu in a room, its wall lit pixel art (`ui/hall-art.ts`, `HallBackdrop`, baked once a size at `ART` screen pixels a pixel and lit per pixel through its slopes like the Library's nave, at most every 70 ms: `planks`, the Smithy's worn oak boards; `vault`, the Study's ashlar between two piers (`pierWidth`: 12 art pixels, 6 in a phone-wide room; the loading screen's only the left, `piers`), each outlined in black and darkened toward its sides so it reads round (`pillarShade` in `library/ashlar.ts`, shared with the nave's piers), its torch hung off its inner side (`.chamber-vault .sconce`, and the loading screen's `.loading-torch`, placed from the `--pier` width `fit` sets on the room); `keep`, the Tiles hall's ashlar with a great round-headed window, pale clear/blue/cream glass with a symmetric rose, pointed lights and diamond panes (the original four-lobed clear window with Pixel Art off), showing sky, hills and the city by `daylight` from `library/render.ts`, and soft moving sunlight with drifting dust), a torch on each side (`paintSconce`, pixel sprites flickering with `getTorchFlicker`/`getTorchSway`) whose light plays over it, and a dim veil (`.chamber-dim`) so text stays readable. A `Descent` takes the Mine or the Library down to its room: the page's three layers (`.descent-world`, `.descent-strata` with `paintStrata`'s far earth and near ladder moving at different speeds, `.descent-chamber`) slide up two heights over `DESCENT_MS`; Reduce motion cuts. `above`/`below` tell the frame loop what to draw. The Tiles tab (`ui/tiles-page.ts`, `TilesPage`) is the keep's hall, hung in the same oak frame as the Defend board and the Mine's and the Library's views (`.tiles-view`): `src/tiles.ts` (pure) makes every palette item and the consumables (`CONSUMABLES`: the bomb, `save.defend.bombs`, and the war banner, one that is never used up) tiles of four types (`TILE_TYPE`, `TILE_FILTERS`: City, Barracks, Towers, Consumables), counts each kind (`tileStacks`, `tileStack`: count, placed, ready), lists its copies with the upgrades each carries (`tileCopies`: placed ones' paths, then `kept`, then plain; keyed `group#n`, the group `kind` or `kind~path~rank`), stacks copies alike (`tileGroups`) and marks their Knowledge upgrades (`tileMarks`: a `became` for each kind grown through from `rootOf`, an evolved one's crowned path's ranks first, then its own path's ranks; the walls' shared `wallRanks`; `marksText` in words); `universal` kinds (no per-building tree: city pieces, walls, consumables) are always one stack, every other kind spread a tile a copy by default or stacked by its upgrades; sells them (`shopOffer`, `canBuyTile`, `buyTile`: metal, bombs with Copper; only the city's pieces and the basic buildings), finds a kind's topic (`tileTopic`, for the Smithy and Study links) and its higher tier (`higherTier`: the path whose crown evolves it). The page shows Collection or Shop, a filter strip, and tiles (each with its marks row, `.tile-marks`, and its path badge and look on its face) in a grid with `grid-auto-flow: dense`, so an opened tile's panel (the tapped copy's or stack's basic stats, then each Knowledge rank it carries as an ability in its path's colour, `buildingSheet`; Buy, the copy's Upgrade tree, the links) spans the row below it; a press held on a tile for `HOLD_MS` turns to Defend with its copy's tree open (`tileTree`, `tileCopyAt` in `src/tiles.ts`; `AppContext.openTree`, `DefendPage.openTree`, refused mid-battle); every relayout animates through `ui/flip.ts` (`flip`: keyed elements slide from their old places, copies grow out of their stack's and gather back into it through an `alias`, leavers fly in `.tiles-ghosts`). `tests/tiles.test.ts`.

**Sound and flourishes.** Recorded sounds live in `public/assets/sounds/` (`ui/`, `battle/`, `collapse/`, `water/`, `ambience/`), named for what they are; one-shots are peak-normalized, loops loudness-matched and trimmed to about 100 s. `src/sound-files.ts` lists them (`ONE_SHOTS`, variants picked at random; `BEDS`), fetches and decodes each on first use (one-shots preloaded once audio starts) and bakes every bed into a seamless loop (`crossfadeLoop`/`blendLoop`: its last `LOOP_FADE` seconds blended equal-power into its start), handing out null while loading or after a failed load; `tests/sound-files.test.ts` checks every file is listed and the seam. `src/sound.ts`'s `SAMPLED` maps cues to recordings (button `knock`, `blast`/`bigBlast`, `arrowHit`, `punch`, `fireballHit`, `meteorHit`, `bodyFall`, `houseFall`, `stoneFall`, `keepFall`, `splash`, `sinking`), falling back to the made voice while loading (cues without one stay silent), at most `MAX_VOICES` recordings at once. Everything else is made in code with Web Audio: `play(cue)` builds each `Cue` (the oak buttons' `knock`, the tab row's `stone`, `coin` for spending, `chime`/`unlock` for skills, `wave`, `record`, `levelUp` (an upgrade point), `trained` (a Smithy upgrade done), the boss wave's `horn`, the fallen keep's `fallen`, and the battle's `arrow`, `cannon`, `blast`, `fireball`, `frost`, `zap`, `swish`, `thunk`, `crumble`, `hammer`, `hiss`, `firework`, with `place` and `banner` for the city's drags) from noise bursts, oscillators and bell partials, into a generated stone-hall echo, at an optional loudness, each battle cue spaced by its `GAP`, and stays silent with the `soundOff` setting. `defend/battle-sound.ts` (`BattleSound`) cues the battle each frame from the shots, flames, bolts, stabs and effects the sim pushed since the last (a WeakSet of those heard), louder the more there were, and the map's other changes as rebuilding taps; it also hears what landed (arrows and fireballs gone from their lists), melee strokes (a melee troop's `cd` jumping up), meteor impacts, boat pools, sinkings, buildings falling by kind (house, stone, keep; from each building's HP last frame) and the city's troops and people dying; presentation only. `src/ambience.ts` uses longer noise beds, broadband rain impacts, irregular insect bouts and sparse, quiet animal calls to reduce tonal and repetitive nature sounds. It is the background: recorded beds (`Scene.land` picks the forest, frozen or desert wind to carry `wind`, the made wind then only filling in; `Scene.mine` the old workings, by `MineRenderer.depthInView` in the Mine and in Defend's underground areas; recorded fires under `fire`), each started looping at a random point once loaded, and looping beds generated once (rain with its drops and a downpour's roar, wind and a blizzard's howl through wandering band-passes, blowing sand, crickets, a fire's roar) and scattered voices (birdsong, an owl, thunder, cave drips, a fire's crackle), each easing toward the `Scene` the showing page reports every frame through `hear` (`DefendPage.ambience` from the area's weather, the boss night and blazes; `MinePage.ambience` from `sim.sky`, its fires, and `thunder` for each fresh strike; `LibraryPage.ambience`, muffled through the stone, its fire unmuffled); it waits for the first press, stays silent with `soundOff` or `ambienceOff` and while the page is hidden. `src/ui/flourish.ts` draws the DOM celebrations: `sparks` (embers off a pressed button, gold for a win, arcane for an upgrade point) and `replay` (restarts a CSS animation class); none play under Reduce motion. `main.ts` knocks and sparks on every button click, glints a rising currency and plays the upgrade point and Smithy cues; the Defend page and skill trees play their own. The animations themselves are in `theme.css`'s Motion section, off when the root has `.reduce-motion` (set from the setting) or the system prefers reduced motion. Both draw from `stream("effects")`.

**Torch light toolkit.** `src/lighting.ts` (the candle colour ramp, flicker and sway: `getTorchFlicker`, `getTorchSway`, `LIGHTING_CONFIG`), `src/torch-light.ts` (`lightFalloff`, `glowColor`, and the occlusion-aware baked glow `torchLightField`/`bakeTorchLight`) and `src/visibility.ts` (`Torch`, `TORCH_LIGHT`, `computeVisibilityPolygon`, `insidePolygon`) are kept from the dungeon game for Defend and future use; `defend/lighting.ts` builds the city's lights on them.

**Saves.** Top-level battle Gold has been removed; legacy coin balances are ignored without conversion. `src/save.ts` loads defensively: `defaults()` defines every field and `decode()` keeps only what is well formed (a newer version starts afresh; Knowledge was once `valor`, a version 1 save's battle copper and silver (once `ironBar` and `steelBar`) join the mine's metal, whose points it multiplies by ten as `BARS_PER_POINT` went from 100 to 10 (`OLD_BARS_PER_POINT` still bounds a saved mine's `worked`); a new game starts with `STARTING_METAL`; a save from before upgrade points gets one for each wave of its best, and a rank in training from before the Smithy counts as done). When adding save state, add it to `defaults()`, the `Save` type and `decode()`. The mine's grid and plan are stored run-length encoded as base64 (`encodeGrid`/`decodeGrid`), as is its water layer (optional, so older saves load dry), and `decodeMineSave` drops a mine unless it is whole (its burning cells, losses, mercy, miners' meals, names, trades and supplies, the allocation, the forge's and smithy's stock (old saves' iron reads as copper), the prospect, the lost, `workedOut`, the building levels, supplies and rebuilds are checked when present, and a mine saved before levels gets levels with room for its crew and trades; a mine saved before the world was widened, 128 cells across, keeps its crew and stock and starts a fresh prospect of its seed); the library's book slots use the same encoding (`decodeLibrarySave`). Player settings are one row each in `SETTINGS` (`src/settings.ts`), from which the `Settings` type, defaults and decoder come; `tests/settings.test.ts` checks every row round-trips.

Shared tab icons live in `src/ui/tab-icons.ts`: pure `tabIconPixels(name)` returns the chosen transparent 16×16 art, and `paintTabIcon` paints it at 3×. An optional variant selects an original review study. Seth chose Defend A (tower/shield), Defend C for Ambush (castle gate), Settings B (steel cog) and a new sword for Siege. `settings.legacyGraphics` defaults false and is exposed as Show legacy graphics (translated in all nine catalogs); existing saves keep the current art. `buildShell` accepts the initial preference, `CombatTab` reads it on refresh, and main refreshes the Settings icon when it changes. Both sets keep the slide animation. `data-tab-icon` is separate from the world-art `data-icon`, so Pixel Art changes cannot overwrite UI icons. `mode-icons.ts`'s drawn icons are the default (the old `assets/ui/defend.png`/`settings.png` are no longer shown in the tab row); only the chosen legacy set uses these canvases. `tests/tab-icons.html` / `tab-icons-view.ts` show chosen icons and all 12 studies; `node tests/tab-icons.mjs` checks desktop/phone rendering, both icon sets, immediate switching, saved preference and Pixel Art independence, and writes screenshots. `node tests/combat-tab.mjs` covers cycling, rapid clicks, reload and Reduce Motion.
