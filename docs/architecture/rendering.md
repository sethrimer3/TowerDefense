# Rendering and art

Board drawing, lighting, outlines/contours, sprites and artist overrides, scenery (forest, city life, water), effects and render performance.

Moved from `AGENTS.md`; keep it current with the code it describes.

**City night life.** `defend/city-torches.ts` derives a repeatable per-fixture ±5-second ignition offset from the shared solar time at near-zero sun-shadow strength; dawn turns lights off. `DefendLighting.burn` combines this with native loss/dousing. Exterior stone-wall sconces sit beyond the sprite (including its hanging southern face). `city-life.ts` owns bounded, aesthetic residents outside `DefendSim`: townsfolk choose building-side doors while no enemies are present; watchmen patrol day and night alike, inside the walls or, when an intact stone gate exists, round the outside. A sampled enemy-cell field triggers shelter inside or offscreen escape outside. Native pathfinding uses a separate friendly mask with a six-route/frame budget; battle time pauses the movement, Reduce Motion freezes it, and presentation hashes never consume sim RNG. `city-life-art.ts` draws townsfolk as a single art pixel in a tunic hashed from their id inside a quarter-art-pixel black ring (`drawTownsfolk`, `TOWNSFOLK_TUNICS`, `TOWNSFOLK_PX`) and watchmen as one flat 2 × 2 art-pixel square (`WATCHMAN_PX`) holding a spear drawn in half art pixels upright at its right side (`watchmanRects`, `WATCHMAN_ART`, `drawWatchman`, which also levels and jabs the spear at a foe; no baked outline, a code-drawn half-pixel ring round each) on whole canvas pixels so they glide rather than step an art pixel at a time, and adds half-strength hand-torch light. No unit in Defend draws a held torch (city life or battle): their glow is the lighting's hand-torch light alone, the torch implied. `sync`'s optional `watchmen` (`DrawOptions.watchmen`, the keep's City watch count in Defend) sets how many walk. **City watch.** In a Defend battle the watchmen fight: `DefendPage.startRun` sets `Bonuses.watch` (`keep-upgrades.ts`'s `cityWatch`; only Defend sets it, so fixtures, Ambush and Siege play as before) and `musterWatch` (`defend/city-watch.ts`) puts the keep's guards (Soldier kind `"watchman"`, `WATCHMAN`, home the keep) out where the watchmen stood; `stepCityWatch` trains a fallen one back every `drill` seconds while the keep stands. They fight like swordsmen (`stepSwordsman`, citywide leash, no deployment range, spear `reach`) and with nothing to fight walk their rounds (`patrolPath`: hashed street spots, `WATCH_PATROL`). `CityLife` hides its watchmen while the sim has the watch and moves them with the guards, so they walk on from there after. `battle-art.ts` draws the guards with `drawWatchman`. Tests: `tests/defend-city-watch.test.ts`, `node tests/city-watch.mjs`. Build and battle scenes share night lighting; cosmetic gate opening never mutates combat collision. `shadow-art.ts` keeps the keep's varied roof heights while filling its actual padded silhouette. Lighting caches binary native-art-pixel body/rim occlusion and invalidates only lights near changed pixels, replacing whole-cell square blocking. No save, balance or replay changes. Tests: `tests/defend-city-life.test.ts`, `PLAYWRIGHT_EXECUTABLE=/usr/bin/chromium node tests/city-night-life.mjs` (native masks, ignition, exterior glow and castle screenshots at desktop/phone sizes). Render-cache parity shares current bakes with the frozen compositor; the city fixture separately checks cutout occlusion.

Forest crowns cache unlit native pixels and apply current darkness in one reused native-size scratch canvas per draw (`forest-art.ts`), so solar phase changes and nearby hand-torches remain visible with Reduce Motion/still air. Never bake a changing light into a motion-dependent crown cache.

**Crisp pixel zoom.** `settings.crispZoom` (default on; Settings' Crisp pixel zoom and the Defend board's own bottom-right Crisp pixels checkbox, `#defend-crisp`, through the host's `crispZoom`/`setCrispZoom`) sets `DefendRenderer.crisp`: the board's scale `px` becomes a whole multiple of `ART` device pixels a cell (at least one device pixel an art pixel, so on a low-density screen the board may overflow its view a little and pan), the zoom (`want`, `snap`) steps so every art pixel covers the same whole number of device pixels, and the camera's offset is rounded to whole device pixels, so each building/world-art outline remains exactly one art pixel wide at every zoom. `damage-art.ts` adds padding outside the logical sprite box; `drawSprite` preserves that box and its origin. Unit contours keep their separate half-art-pixel width and fractional coverage. Off, the zoom is smooth as before. Ambush, Siege and previews keep the smooth zoom.

**Board resolution and Ultra graphics.** `settings.resolution` (choice 0 = the screen's own, 2, 1.5, 1; Settings' Board resolution select under Crisp pixel zoom) caps the device pixels a CSS pixel the battle board draws at: `DefendRenderer.maxDpr` (set by Defend, Ambush and Siege before `resize`; `dpr` the capped ratio) sizes the canvas and `px` from it, and the browser scales it up (`image-rendering: pixelated`); pointer maths are ratios, so nothing else changes. `settings.ultraGraphics` (off by default) turns on the costliest effects: today the torchlight sheen on outlines, `DrawOptions.sheen` gating `DefendLighting.drawLight`'s building-rim `shine` and `UnitOutline.shineContours`. Absent `sheen` counts as on, so fixtures and `test:render-cache` keep the full drawing. Presentation only.

**Tile grid preferences.** `settings.ts` defines `gridOpacity` (0–100 in 5% steps, default 30) and `gridColor` (white, brown, black, gray, gold; default gold for existing artwork). The shared range decoder snaps old or imported values to the nearest step. Settings shows both controls while Tile grid lines is on; `main.ts` passes their saved values through `DefendHost` to `DrawOptions`/`drawGrid`. Zero opacity skips the grid even during dragging; placement previews keep their own styling. `node tests/grid-settings.mjs` checks desktop/phone controls, keyboard steps, rendering, persistence and zero opacity (dev server required).

**Water and winter ice.** `boats.ts` indexes all pools, filtering decorative or frozen pools out of liquid combat checks. In cold areas (or debug snow), water freezes at its current radius and every boat leaves persistent frozen sheets, including small boats. Quantized, deduplicated winter emissions bound stationary trails. Fireballs, burning ground, wizard flames, dragon breath and explosions thaw intersecting sheets into water with a fresh eight-second lifetime; melted sheets never refreeze. Ice survives later area changes for the rest of the battle. `icyCell` blocks builder jobs and final rebuilding wherever ice overlaps rubble. `ice-motion.ts` retains ground-unit momentum, adds blast impulses on ice, and checks collisions along slides. Fliers and boats do not slide. Natural ponds freeze during cold weather and heat opens liquid cells (`thawedPonds`); their permanent pond water remains. Surfaces are transient battle state, not saved. `flood-art.ts` draws softer banks, separate reflections for disconnected spans, tree reflections, bounded movement wakes and rain rings; ice has fixed facets and cracks. `pond-water.ts` adds footstep rings and frozen pond art. Reduce Motion disables rings and shimmering; disabling park effects still leaves gameplay ice visible. Run `tests/defend-ice.test.ts` and `node tests/defend-water.mjs` (dev server required, desktop/mobile galleries in `test-results/`). Water buffer checks compare reused storage with fresh current art because the visuals intentionally differ from the frozen reference.

**The forest.** Mossbound Ruins (`FOREST_AREAS`, `hasForest` in `defend/forest.ts`) fills the open ground outside the walls (`ForestPlan.open`) with the parks' grass, large, larger and huge trees seen from straight above (canopies only, no trunks; the canopies' middles carry no ring or rim, which read as a square), a few small berry bushes (`FOREST.bushes`, size 0 `bush` trees with red berries), rocks, dirt pads, a few lakes and boulders. All but the boulders are presentation only: nothing walks round them or is slowed. **Boulders** (`boulderPlan`, cached per map, `BOULDERS`: first `mountains` small mountains of `peak` cells, then lone stones, 2 × 2 blocks, clusters of three to six and heaps of seven to twelve, each grown toward its most hemmed-in cell so it stays round with any ground it closes round filled; every cell `clear` cells from anything but open ground, `apart` from each other and out of the spawn rows above `top`, so they never wall ground in) are gameplay: `DefendSim.syncBoulders` (each step) sets `sim.rocks` and makes them solid like ponds (owned by no building, `impassable` in the enemies' and the banner's fields) while `hasForest(areaForWave(wave))`, and opens them again on leaving the area; shots pass over them (`lineOfSight`). `forestPlan(map)` (cached per map) lays the lakes (discs, `FOREST.lakes`) first, at spots tried in a hashed order, glades first, then the large, larger and huge trees (`TreeSize` 1 to 3, `FOREST.big`), then the berry bushes in the open between them, then puts an ordinary tree on an `OUT` cell by each cell's own hashes and a value-noise thicket field (`FOREST`), oak, pine or bush in `VARIANTS` looks, only where its canopy (`canopyRadius`) and `FOREST.clear` keep off every other cell (`clearOf`) and off the lakes, big trees and boulders, so a placed structure clears only the trees it stands on; rocks and pads fill the cells left, pads mostly in the glades. `ForestDamage` (fresh each run; `state` per tree: standing, burning, down) brings trees down, found through a per-cell index: `hit` (any attack landing under a tree no bigger than `FRAIL`) and `cut` (a charge or dash, same sizes) strike them apart into a `Stump` (sawn clean, `cut`, by blades), `blast` (any size) blows them up into a `Crater` with no stump and bares the grass round it, and `ignite`/`igniteAlong` (any size) set them `burning` for `BURN` seconds, after which `burnOut` leaves an `Ash` pile on a scorched (`burnt`) clearing, no stump; `clear` (every blast, burning ground and the Hero's spins) bares the grass to dirt, a `Clearing` (only over open ground not bared already, at most `MAX_CLEARINGS`). `defend/forest-art.ts` (`ForestArt`, kept by the renderer): `update` sets alight what landed fire touches (fireballs and fire arrows gone from their lists, then new blazes and wizard flames, before the blasts so a fireball's own boom leaves its trees burning), blasts with every new `boom` effect and the Hero's spins, cuts with dashes and the valkyries' stabs (a WeakSet of those seen), strikes apart with arrows and ballista bolts that landed (`landed`, tracked in flight), lightning's points and melee strokes (`strokes`: a melee troop's or walking enemy's cooldown jumping up, checked only on cells a frail tree covers, `frailOver`), burns the burning out (`drawBurning` draws each as `charPixels`/`charSprite`, its leaves charring black with embers in four steps, under a glow and flickering pixel flame tongues; embers and smoke rise off it, `smoulder`), leaves `AshPile`s (`ashPile`: grains placed by the ash's hashes, drawn by `drawFloor` over the ground, pushed out of the way by anyone on foot, `trample`), stamps `stumpSprite`s (sawn rings or splintered shards, bark and root flares, outlined) with `chips` round them into the floor, throws leaves, clumps and splinters (`burst`, battle time, from `defendRandom("effects")`, none under Reduce Motion), startles birds out of the trees it shoots near, blasts near, sets alight, fells or has walked under (`forest-birds.ts`, `Birds`: about half the trees roost a few, `roosting`, by id; they climb and flap away from the disturbance with their shadows, drawn over the trees, and return after `BIRDS.roost` battle seconds; none under Reduce Motion) and fades trees to `UNDER_ALPHA` over anyone under them (a per-cell bucket of `beneath`); `drawFloor` (in `drawCity`, under the units and lighting) keeps the ground's pixels (`paintFloor`: the parks' grass patches and tufts on every open cell (`lawnIn`; `turfAt` marks what is still grass, `shadedAt` the trees' shadows), pads, the clearings' dirt, lakes (`lakeDepth`: banks and outline, water dithered from teal shallows over sand to the deep, the bank's shadow from the upper left, a foam lip opposite, ripple lines, lily pads and reeds), craters with stumps, rocks, boulders, heaps of `MOUNTAIN` cells or more as small mountains (`mountain`: the cells blended into a rounded outline, a height field from the distance in (`chamfer`) lit from the upper left, moss and scree, crags heaped on top and a long shadow), the standing trees' shadows from the upper left) and repaints only the boxes round felled trees and clearings, kept apart unless they overlap; `GroundRelief`'s flagstone light fades out with the forest, whose grass hides them; `drawWater` twinkles the lakes' glints (`waterGlints`) on the wall clock, still under Reduce Motion; `drawTrees` (after the parks' trees, darkened the same way) composes the standing trees' pixels (`composeCrowns`, run lists per sprite) one of `BANDS` row bands a frame, each tree in the lean frame `leanOf` gives (`treeSprite`: per size, more whorls and clumps the bigger, five frames, the canopy sheared, the trunk still; wind by weather, none under Reduce Motion or with effects off). `forest-grass.ts` (`ForestGrass`, kept by the renderer, drawn after the floor while effects are on) grows the parks' waving blades (`park-grass.ts`'s `paintBlade`, `Blade`, `CURVE`, `PALETTE32` with its `SHADOW` half) over every open cell (`forestBlades`: none on lakes, boulders, rocks, pads or under the big crowns), only where the floor is still `turfAt`, darkened where `shadedAt`, parting round at most `CROWD` walkers a cell (typed-array buckets), swayed from sines baked per blade (`Swaying`) so no trigonometry runs per blade a frame; one board-sized buffer at most 30 times a second. The renderer fades it in and out with the area crossfade (`forestShare`). The floor covers the open ground the wall's brick face hangs over, so the renderer copies those strips back from the city layer over it (`hangingFaces` in `city-layer.ts`, `drawFaces`), repaints the wall spikes' points past the wall (`paintSpikePoints`), and no blade roots under a face (`ForestGrassFrame.faced`). Run `tests/defend-forest.test.ts` and `node tests/defend-forest.mjs` (dev server required; isolated saves).

**Simple unit squares.** The Settings page's Simple square enemies and Simple square troops (`simpleEnemies`, `simpleTroops`, through the host and `DrawOptions`) make `drawUnits` hand enemies or troops to `simple-units.ts` (`drawSimpleEnemies`, `drawSimpleTroops`): each a square of its catalog size and colour (white while struck) in a one-art-pixel black outline (gold while marked, dark red for bosses), lit top and left and shaded bottom and right, batched into one path per colour and edge so crowds cost a few fills. Their sprites, marks and swords are skipped; the combat cache key includes both. The visibility contours below replace the rims for player/Dark One units in production. Presentation only.

**Placed pieces' shimmer.** While the build palette is open (`DrawOptions.shimmer`, off under Reduce Motion), `placed-shimmer.ts` (`drawShimmer`) now and then lights a glowing gold line round each piece the player placed (structures and the keep, gates, wall ballistas, placed wall sections, those touching edge to edge as one piece (`wallGroups`) traced round its outer edge only (`groupRing`): `shimmerTargets`, cached per map), clockwise from its top left, brightening to full as it goes, then fading (`shimmerAt`, `SHIMMER`). The line is one layer of art pixels touching the piece's sprite edge to edge (`outlineRing`, so corners are cut): a structure's ring is read once a kind, size, look and `pixelKey()` from its own pixels, painted alone through `paintStanding`; gates and ballistas fill their cells, so what can be moved stands out from the houses and wall ring the city grows itself. Pieces share one wall-clock cycle (`shimmerPeriod`: at least `SHIMMER.every` seconds, longer in big cities so one starts about every `gap` seconds), their starts spread by the golden ratio so they take turns. The Display setting Always highlight selectable buildings (`highlightBuildings`, off by default, with `highlightOpacity` 5–100% and `highlightWalls`/`highlightRealm` shown under it while on; the host's `highlight`, `DrawOptions.highlight`) replaces the shimmer with `drawHighlights`: a steady, unanimated outline round every selectable building (`highlightTargets`: the shimmer's targets plus the keep; Realm pieces are the keep, gates, wall ballistas and the Realm palette's buildings), in build and battle, leaving fallen ones out. Presentation only. Run `tests/defend-shimmer.test.ts` and `node tests/defend-shimmer.mjs` (dev server required; isolated saves).

**Legacy merged unit outlines.** The Settings page's Merged unit outlines (`mergedOutlines`, on by default, through the host and `DrawOptions`) hand `drawUnits` a `UnitOutline` (`src/defend/unit-outline.ts`, kept by the renderer): civilians, troops and enemies are drawn into its unit layer and every black outline into an outline layer under all of their bodies, then both are copied onto the board, so touching or overlapping units share one outline round their combined shape. The unit layer's `drawImage` draws each sprite `bare` (its outer ring of near-black pixels taken off, `stripOutline`) and puts its `silhouette` (solid pixels in `OUTLINE`) on the outline layer, unless drawn fainter than `SOLID`; both copies are made once a sprite (`liveSprite` marks canvases repainted in place, the Hero's cape) and the outline layer mirrors the unit layer's transforms. The rims `battle-art.ts` draws by hand (guild troops, weapons, fists, fallback squares, bosses) go through `rimOf`, and the simple squares' outlines through `rims`; fliers' shadows and shields go on the board beneath. Plain enemy squares without a sprite now have a black rim. Swords, projectiles and effects draw after, unmerged; the combat cache key includes the setting. Presentation only. Run `tests/defend-unit-outline.test.ts`; `PERF_OUTLINES=0` turns it off in `tests/defend-performance.mjs`.

**The wilds.** Beyond the board's four edges the renderer draws the area's impassable scenery (`defend/wilds.ts`, `Wilds`, drawn in `DefendRenderer.draw` right after the beyond-the-board fill) so it carries on the field's own look with no seam. `wildsPlan` lays it out once, in cells, `MARGIN` (72, nine tiles) all round: trees by the forest's own rules (`thicket`, `FOREST.big`) thinning out from two tiles to six, rocks as sparse as the field's, and whole mountain ranges from a tile or so out wherever `rangeAt` (the rise with distance plus slow noise) passes 0, so forest valleys reach into them and the odd lone peak stands in front. `paintWilds` paints the still ground once per area into a cached canvas at `ART` pixels a cell (`ground`, about 0.7 s): in the forest areas the field's own lawn (`lawnCell`), then the ranges (`paintRanges` over `rangeField`, one cached height field: a foot where `rangeAt` crosses 0 plus four ridged `smoothNoise` octaves turned against each other, sampled every 4 art pixels and blended so the finest octave's facets make the rock jagged, lit from the upper left in the forest's crag greys with dithered moss on the gentler slopes, outlined; in the forest, groves of still bushes and small trees where the moss noise is high on the gentler slopes), the field's rocks (`rockSprite`) with their shadows, and the trees' shadows; elsewhere the stone is recoloured per area (`STONES`, `recolour`), except in the Crystal Caves, whose wilds carry the cavern's gravel on past the board (`caveGravelPixel`, its trails with `caveTrailPixel`, the floor under the great opening lit by day and moonlit by night through the `caveOpening` layers, so the field's edge is no seam; the field's own opening moonlight is clipped to the board) up to basalt (`paintBasalt`: jittered hexagonal columns, nearest of a staggered grid of `COLUMN`-pixel centres, standing in stepped clusters to the ranges' height, tops lit from the upper left with a pale rim, dark cracks and the shade of taller columns, sides showing south over lower columns or the floor, faceted and darkening to their foot, purple and blue moss in patches dripping down the sides, small mushrooms in the moss), and in the desert, whose ranges are Moab sandstone (`paintMesas` over `mesaField`: the height box-blurred into rounded slickrock and stepped every `MOAB_STEP` into strata whose edges wander, each a flat bench lit from the upper left in `MOAB_STRATA`, dark red at the foot to buff caprock, with faint cross-bedding and sand drifted into hollows of the lower benches, every step down to the south a `MOAB_FACE`-pixel red cliff face, bedded, darkening to its foot and streaked with desert varnish, outlined in dark red-brown; their long shadows are cast from the stepped strata, ragged at the rim but never on a bench except from a higher cliff) over the area's own flagstones, drawn tile by tile with `paintFloorTile` from `city-layer.ts`. In the forest with effects on, `life` repaints the strips in view at up to 30 fps: the field's swaying grass blades (`planCell`, `planBlades`) and the trees leaning in the wind (`leanOf`), with the field's own trees that overhang its edge drawn over them. In the Amber Desert, while the battle's dunes are drawn, the field's own dune tile (`duneTile`, in the dunes' current light) runs on over the wilds' flagstones right up to the sandstone ranges, with no wilds rocks scattered on it, and each sand trail crossing the top or bottom edge carries on (`roadReach`, cut and banked by `trail-art.ts`'s `sandReachCanvases` exactly as on the field) until it disappears under the mountains; `Wilds.sand` repaints that canvas only when the dunes relight or the trails change. Beside every sand trail, on the field (`AtmosphereArt.taperCanvas`, also lowering `SandMarks.taper` so marks read the same sand) and in the wilds, the dunes run down to it (`duneTaperPixels`, `TAPER`: the swell's height eased to the flat sheet over a few half widths of `trailField`'s distance, which now reaches `FIELD_REACH`), so a slip face slopes into the trail rather than ending in a cliff. The swells' cast shadows thin out there in a dither as they sink, and sooner their lit crest lines, so a crest dies away before the trail; the wind ripples, grains and pebbles stay almost to the trail (`rippleDetail`, then `grainDetail` at its edge), so the sinking sand keeps the dunes' texture. The trails' banks (`bankPixels`) are dithered, not a smooth wash. The mountains' shadows multiply the ground by a cool grey (`SHADE_INK`), so over a dune's own shade the two deepen a little together; in the desert they rise and fall gently with the dunes (`mountainShade(duneHeights())`, lifted `DUNE_LIFT`: more and the swells cut them into pools along their troughs). The board's daytime tint (`WildsFrame.tint`, soft light) washes the wilds too. Night darkens it all, more than the field (which keeps its torches), as dark as the board's own ambient at its edge (`WildsFrame.dark`, else `NIGHT_EDGE` of the full darkness) and deepening over `NIGHT_FADE` cells (`nightShade`), so the lit field doesn't end in a step. The mountains also cast long shadows down and right over the wilds and the field alike, whether or not they are on screen: `mountainShade` sweeps the height field once along the diagonal (the tallest caster's height sinking `DROP` a pixel), and `Wilds.drawShade`, called by `DefendRenderer.draw` after the field's trees, lays the cropped mask over the view at a third opacity, fading out at night. Nothing walks there and nothing is saved: it only fills the view, so a window of any shape shows scenery, never black, round the square field. Any battle screenshot shows it.

**Golden motes.** Every upgrade tree has golden motes riding a small fluid round it, after Tower Delve's skill trees (`src/ui/tree-motes.ts`, `TreeMotes`, presentation only, from `stream("effects")`): a 40 × 40 forced fluid (advected, relaxed toward the forcing, its divergence projected out, stepped at most 30 times a second) swirls gently round every upgrade (hard round the one picked) and runs as a current along every branch from an upgrade to the one it opens. Each mote is a square of gold pixels one, two or three across (`SIZES`: their shares, colours, a lit top left pixel on the bigger two, and `follow`, the rate its velocity eases toward the fluid's, a little lower the bigger it is, so big motes swing wider), snapped to a grid of half the branches' art pixel, fading where it wraps across an edge; `burst` pushes them outward from a purchase (in tree coordinates). The fluid and motes live in the tree's own space (`MoteScene.area`, a box in tree coordinates, drawn through `view`, the pan and zoom), so they pan and zoom with it and fill all the ground the view can be panned over, only those on screen drawn. Nothing is drawn under Reduce Motion. `UpgradeGraph` keeps one on its `.ug-motes` canvas between the branches and the nodes (every Defend building, side and map tree, and the Study's War banner and Bomb trees; its area is `panArea`, the nodes' box grown by `PAN_MARGIN` (a fifth) of its size each way, at least `PAN_MIN`, and `clampAxis` keeps a dragged or zoomed view within it, centring an axis the area fits; the view zooms out no further than `ZOOM_OUT` (a quarter) past fitting the tree, and the motes' own area is the pan area widened by `coverView` to cover the view there, so no corner is ever empty; `burst(id)` on `DefendPage.bought` and `Ledger.learn`), and `Ledger` one on each spell path tree's `.path-motes` canvas under its ranks (the canvas itself its area, unpanned), its upgrades read from the page each frame (`pathMoteScene`, drawn from `drawCircle`; bursting on `learnPath`/`evolve`). Run `tests/tree-motes.test.ts` and `node tests/tree-motes.mjs` (dev server required; isolated saves).

**Damage repaints.** `DefendRenderer.refreshLayer` repaints the whole city layer and the lighting's body/rim masks only when the map, scale, area, looks or Pixel Art change, or a building's kind or lot does (`shapeOf`); it keys on what the layer shows (`damageKey` over `buildingState`: the damage stage while standing, else `-1 - built`), never `mapVersion`, which counts each rebuilt cell. When only buildings' looks change (`recordStages`/`damageBoxes`: at most `MAX_DAMAGE_BOXES` (64), under 40% of the board; falls and rises too while the shared sky holds the shadows, else those repaint everything), it repaints boxes `DAMAGE_MARGIN` cells round them through a clip (`repaintBox`), passing `CityScene.within` so `paintCityLayer`/`paintBuildingMask` skip what can't reach the box (`REACH`), and `DefendLighting.patchMasks` rereads just those boxes (`readMasks`, shared with `setMasks`) and rebakes nearby lights; a box where only a fallen building's rebuilt sections changed is `layerOnly` (the masks hold only what stands). `SunShadows` recasts on `standingKey` (falls, rises), at most every `RECAST_MS` (250). `node tests/structure-churn.mjs` (manual; `CHURN_HITS`, `CHURN_PHASE`, `CHURN_PROFILE`) times frames while many buildings are struck, fall and are rebuilt at once. Pixel-exact with Crisp pixel zoom; with a fractional scale a few anti-aliased pixels on box edges may differ by a shade. Presentation only. `node tests/defend-damage-repaint.mjs`.

**Edit repaints.** Placing, moving or taking up a building regenerates the whole city (`generateCity`), but nothing is rebuilt from scratch for it. `map-change.ts`'s `changedCells` marks the cells whose look differs from the last city (type, city/wall flags, bridges, spikes, and each owner building's kind, lot, variant, path look and gate/corner/fence detail), `changeBoxes` groups them into boxes `DAMAGE_MARGIN` wider, and `DefendRenderer.mapBoxes` (build phase, shared sky only) repaints the city layer and body/rim masks through `repaintBox` as the damage repaints do; past `MAX_DAMAGE_BOXES` or 40% of the board it repaints everything. `DefendLighting.setMap(map, changed)` keeps the bake of every light standing exactly where it stood (matched by kind, position, radius and owner lot, not id), rebakes only those near the change and returns the lights that came or went (their brackets' cells join the boxes); so torches never go dark on an edit. A torch's dusk offset follows its position, not its id. `patchMasks` redraws the drawn-up masks, their tint and grown copy only inside the boxes. `repaintBox` clips to the pixels wholly inside a box. `noteEdit`/`editedFrom` (a weak lineage) let per-city bakes patch from the last city's: `groundArt` repaints the street cells within two of a change, `ParkGrass.sync` replans cells within four. `parkArt` is shared between cities whose park, pond and bridge cells match (`parksKey`), so park trees, ponds and ducks carry on. With a fractional board scale the browser samples a pixel-art canvas drawn through a clip a pixel off here and there, so the layer may differ from a whole repaint in a few scattered pixels; masks, bakes, the forest floor and the grass plans match exactly. `node tests/defend-edit-repaint.mjs`.

**Light field.** `DefendLighting.drawLight` no longer draws each light's two swayed bakes scaled up onto the board-sized darkness and glow (four filtered draws a light, a frame). `LightField` (`src/defend/light-field.ts`, pure typed arrays) sums them on the bakes' own lattice (`RES` samples a cell, `FIELD_W` × `FIELD_H`): each bake carves `1 - alpha × level` from the darkness and adds its premultiplied candle colour to the glow, the flame's sub-pixel bob resampled between rows; the result is painted to two field-sized canvases and drawn up to the board once each. A glow past full is painted at a half or a quarter and added onto itself after drawing up (`paintGlow`'s doublings), so saturated pools still spread full brightness into their edge pixels. Hand torches are summed on their own field layer (a 1024-step falloff table) and drawn up. What stands is read from the buildings' own art, not their cells: `render.ts`'s `paintMasks` paints `paintBuildingMask` (`city-layer.ts`, through `damage-art.ts`'s `withMask`: `body` the original outline-free sprite pixels, `rim` the shared outside rings plus the joined standing wall's outline) at `MASK` (16) pixels a cell whenever the city layer repaints, and `DefendLighting.setMasks` takes them: a cell casts shadows in a bake only while the body covers half of it (`coverage`), so light runs over a lot's open edges right up to the sprite; `unlightBodies` cuts the glow out of the bodies and lays the night's darkness back over them (roofs stay unlit); and the glow kept to the inner strip of the rims (`hug`: within a quarter art pixel of the body) is added over them at `LIGHTING_CONFIG.glow.rim` (0.36, `shine`), so outlines in torchlight shine faintly warm, flickering with the torches as the glow is summed afresh each frame. Units' contours shine the same way (`UnitOutline.shineContours`, `shineOutlines`, the inner half beside the unit bodies) on frames the lighting is drawn. With no masks yet, torches fall back to the cell ground mask. It is close to the old drawing, not exact: where pools meet a wall's edge they are multiplied before they are smoothed, so those edge pixels differ by up to about 30 of 255 (mean about 1). `tests/defend-light-field.test.ts` checks the arithmetic; `test:render-cache` pins main's current lighting arithmetic from `c10cafb` exactly with actual body/rim masks; the earlier whole-cell lighting tolerance comparison is superseded. It also checks the trees' shading box exactly. `ParkTrees` clears, shades (`source-atop`, clipped) and copies back only the box round the trees.
