# Defend buildings and upgrades

Basic buildings, attunements, branch buildings, ultimates, side trees (keep, walls, gate, ballistas), repair shops and placed-building orders.

Moved from `AGENTS.md`; keep it current with the code it describes.

**Basic buildings and per-building upgrades.** The shop sells only the city's pieces and three basic buildings (`BASIC_BUILDINGS` in `catalog.ts`): the Watch tower, the Melee Guild (brawlers, `BRAWLER`, who punch through the swordsman's code) and the Ranged Guild (stone throwers, `SLINGER`, through the archer's, shooting `Arrow.shot` stones). Every other tower and troop building is one of those upgraded, one building at a time, with Knowledge (`src/specializations.ts`). Every line has one shape (`ATTUNED` in `specializations.ts`): a basic building becomes an attuned one (Watch tower into Archer, Cannon or Wizard tower; Melee Guild into Barracks, Assassins' den or Spearmen hall; Ranged Guild into Archer barracks, Mage Guild or Gunner hall), which takes one of three exclusive branches of three ranks; a branch with `via` starts with its own attunement node, a `become` step that makes it another building (the Wizard tower's Pyre, Frost and Storm towers; the Spearmen hall's Phalanx, Javelin camp and Lancer stable; the Gunner hall's Musket hall, Machine-gun nest and Rocket battery), which then climbs the branch's three ranks. Every attuned line's branches now have one. The wizard's branch towers (`WIZARD_TOWERS`, `isWizardTower` in `catalog.ts`) all cast through `wizard.ts`, whose `paths` reads what each casts from its kind (the Pyre tower flames only, the Frost tower ice only and twice as often, the Storm tower bolts through `STORM.links[0]` enemies for flames) and its path's rank for the rest; their paths' topics are the towers (`pyreTower`, `frostTower`, `stormTower`), `decodeDefendSave`'s `splitBranches` turns a saved wizard tower or barracks wearing one into its branch's building, and `decodePaths` still reads the legacy topic-wide `wizardTower` and `barracks` choices (`LEGACY_TOPICS`). Their art is `tower-art.ts`'s `paintBranchTower` (placeholder: the wizard roof in the branch's look with corner pieces, no PNG yet). The barracks' branches likewise make the Chapterhouse (`chapterHouse`, crusaders: `CRUSADE.hp[0]` and slower before any rank), the Berserker lodge (`berserkerLodge`, strokes in `BERSERK.rage` of the time) and the Halberd yard (`halberdYard`, `HALBERD.drill` more reach), all training swordsmen (`SWORD_HALLS`, `isSwordHall`) through `troops.ts`'s `paths`, which reads the hall's kind; the melee cape reads a swordsman's own hall's topic; `paintBranchHall` draws them (placeholder: the barracks in the branch's look with its standards at the door, a third on the ridge once climbing). The Assassins' den's branches make the Blood hall (`bloodHall`, criticals `BLOODLETTERS.hall`), the Veil house (`veilHouse`, cloaked by `SHADOWCLOAK.hall`) and the Poison den (`poisonDen`, `VENOM.hall`), all training assassins (`ASSASSIN_DENS`, `isAssassinDen`); `assassins.ts`'s `denOf` turns a den's kind and ranks into what its assassins do (shared by battle and `kindStats`); Headhunters stays a spare on the den itself; `paintBranchDen` draws them (placeholder: the den in the branch's look with its tokens on the hall, a third in the yard once climbing). The archer tower's branches make the Brazier tower (`brazierTower`, lit arrows, `FIRE_ARROWS.hall`) and the Hawk tower (`hawkTower`, `SHARP.hall` more reach), the cannon tower's the Gun battery (`batteryTower`, `GUNNERY.hall`) and the Mortar tower (`mortarTower`, `SIEGE_SHOT.hall`), all shooting through `towers.ts` (`ARCHER_TOWERS`/`isArcherTower`, `CANNON_TOWERS`/`isCannonTower`; its `paths` reads the kind); `paintBranchTower` takes the base painter, and draws them with corner tokens (placeholder). The archer barracks' branches make the Ranger lodge (`rangerLodge`, `RANGERS.lodge` more sight) and the Skirmish camp (`skirmishCamp`, draws in `SKIRMISH.camp` of the time), training archers (`ARCHER_HALLS`/`isArcherHall`), and the Mage Guild's the Inferno hall (`infernoHall`, fireballs `PYROCLASM.hall` harder) and the Cinder hall (`cinderHall`, burning ground `CINDERS.hall` longer), training fire mages (`MAGE_HALLS`/`isMageHall`; `mages.ts`'s `paths` reads the kind, and a Cinder hall's fireballs carry their `home`); `paintBranchDen` takes the base painter and draws them too (placeholder). The wizard towers' finial is a three-pixel cross on the roof's middle pixel. Until a branch is taken, an attuned building's tree shows only each branch's first node (`climb`'s `peek`), the rest trailing off hidden. Three buildings of one attuned line, each maxed on a different branch, merge into its ultimate (step `merge`, `MERGE_COST` 0, `mergePartners` finds the other two among kept copies then placed ones, which are used up): Wizard tower into the Dark wizard keep, Barracks into the Hero, Assassins' den into the Shadow, Cannon tower into C4 nanobots, Archer barracks into the Sniper lodge, Spearmen hall into the Valkyrie palace, Gunner hall into the Gatling emplacement. `BEYOND` lists what an ultimate can still become (Sniper lodge into the Revolutionary). Lines still awaiting an ultimate (currently the Mage Guild) show locked `soon` nodes (`u:<attuned>`) still to come; a line with two branches merges two. Ultimates keep their own paths. Paths a kind can no longer take stay in `PATHS` with their battle code (`legacyCrown` records old crowns) for fixtures (`tests/spare-paths.ts`'s `wear`, `battleBonuses`), and `buildingPathsOf` keeps them off placements. Undoing a merge node parts the ultimate: it stands as the middle branch, the other two wait as kept copies; a respec of an ultimate gives three basic buildings (`mergedFrom`). Save version 7 (`migrateTrees`) refunds worn ranks on retired paths and the difference between each old ultimate's crown price and its free merge (`OLD_ULTIMATES`). `placeOf(kind)` names a kind's tier. `nextSteps(kind, wear?)` lists what a building can take next and `upgradeBuilding(save, target, step)` (target `{ uid }` or `{ spikes }`) pays the catalog's price (free with Unlimited money): a kind change moves one copy in `owned` and drops any path, standing where it was when its footprint is unchanged and still fits, else taken up to the palette (`waiting`); a path rank sets `PlacedStructure.path/rank` (`SpikeSpot.path/rank`). Path and rank live on the placement and move with it. Taken back to the palette, a building wearing a path becomes a kept copy (`DefendSave.kept`, diffed by `setLayout` through `settleKept`) with its own palette entry (`kind~path~rank`, drawn in its look with its badge); placing it again wears the same. `placedBattlePaths` snapshots each placement's own rank. In Defend, tapping a placed building (or spike row) while building, or pressing and holding it or its palette tile (`BoardPointers` turns a drag held still for `HOLD_MS`, 450 ms, into `PointerHost.hold`; `hasTree` says which kinds have one), opens its upgrade tree (`#defend-specialty`, `openSpecialty`). A palette copy is a `SpecialtyTarget` too (`{ copy, wear? }`, `copyWaits`): `upgradeBuilding`/`respecBuilding` change its `owned` copy or `kept` entry and return the copy it became: `buildingTree(kind, wear)` lists the live nodes (`k:<kind>` buildings, `p:<path>:<rank>` path ranks; owned, next or locked), the lineage it has grown through, then only the branches still open, so a choice drops the branches beside it and adds the ones only it leads to; `ui/specialty.ts`'s `treeGraph` turns them into `GraphNode`s and `treeCardHtml` draws the picked node's card (what it does, its level, Buy). `ui/upgrade-graph.ts` (`UpgradeGraph`, presentation only) draws them as pixel-framed nodes on pixel-art branches laid out by `layout` (a tidy tree of the branches carrying on, root at the bottom, growing up; `GraphNode.side` nodes, `TreeNode.side`: becoming another building, sit left and right of their parent a little below, row under row, the root's odd one straight below), each on springs to its place and its parent and pushing neighbours apart, so dragged nodes pull their branch and ease back; drag the background to pan, the wheel or a pinch to zoom; nodes that leave the tree wither brown and fall away, new ones sprout from their parent (snapped under Reduce Motion). Branches not shown yet trail off a node as lines scattering into pixels (`hidden`, `TreeNode.hidden`, a `BranchSlot` each: what becoming that building or taking that crown would open, fanned away from its parent leaning up) and the branches a choice closed off trail off from an owned building node in withered, drooping, crumbling brown toward where each stood (`closed`, its openings less the one `took`; `trailAim`: a building it could have become beside and below, a path it passed over on the same side of the taken one, which now carries on straight up). Every branch and trail is painted each frame onto one canvas under the nodes (`.ug-branches`, `data-hidden`/`data-closed` counting the trails) by `ui/branch-art.ts` (`BranchArt`, pure: two art pixels thick, lit from the upper left inside a black outline, faded tails broken up by an ordered dither, owned and buyable branches with a faint halo and a travelling spark, locked ones dashed, owned path ranks in their `--hue`), a whole number of device pixels an art pixel (`ART` board pixels at zoom 1); `tests/upgrade-branches.test.ts`. The tree's header has a little Map button (`data-ug-map`, `DefendPage.toggleMap`, `renderMap`; not on the keep's and other side trees) that swaps it for the **upgrade map** of the building's whole line: `fullTree(root)` lists every node the line can reach (buildings, path ranks, crowns and what they evolve into, ids as `buildingTree`'s), `mapGraph`/`mapCardHtml`/`mapCount`/`mapWeb` in `ui/specialty.ts` show those discovered (`foundNodes`: `DefendSave.upgradesFound`, optional, absent with none, decoded by `decodeFound` in `save.ts`, plus `heldNodes`, what the city and palette hold now; `recordFound` runs on every upgrade), this building's own owned, other discoveries `next`, the rest only in Developer mode (`host.devMode()`) as locked, and undiscovered branches trail off as `hidden`; `UpgradeGraph.set(nodes, picked, web)` places them by `webLayout` of the whole web, round the root, so discoveries appear where they belong. Read only. Run `tests/upgrade-map.test.ts` and `node tests/defend-upgrade-map.mjs` (dev server required; isolated saves). Tapping an owned node offers Undo (`undoUpgrade`, `undoTo`, `undoRefund`: it and every upgrade after it, free, back to the node it grew from, through the same `rewind` a respec uses; the keep's and walls' `undoKeep`/`undoWalls`, `sideUndo` in `ui/specialty.ts`, take a rank and those above it). Tapping the picked node again (`DefendPage.again`) buys it when it is next, or, when it is owned, sells everything after it and stays on it (`treeBeyond`: undoes the owned node grown from it; `sideBeyond`: the rank above it on its branch, or on the root every rank, a respec); its card says so (`.ug-again`). Its top left box shows the Knowledge `allocated(kind, wear)` to the building (every step from its basic building, at today's prices); Respec (`respecBuilding`, free, one press) gives it all back and turns the building back into its basic one with no path. The host's `game`/`spentKnowledge` let it pay and save. Tiles shows an opened tile's basic stats and its upgrades' abilities (`buildingSheet` in `ui/specialty.ts`), and a press held on a tile opens its tree in Defend (`tileTree`); the shop refuses upgraded kinds. The Study researches only skills and strike spells: building paths are `sealed` in `pathState`, and `learnPath`/`evolve` refuse them. Save version 5: `migrateResearch` (every load) refunds all building path research as Knowledge (crowns too), leaves the city's buildings plain (a path without a rank is dropped) and turns evolved buildings back into the ones they grew from (those standing are taken up), so every rank a building wears was paid for on it and a respec gives back exactly that; an unfinished building research job is refunded. Version 3's `defend.cards` (`migrateCards`) and version 2's topic-wide `save.paths` (`migrateTopicPaths`) still migrate onto placements first. `ui/path-badge.ts` draws a worn rank's badge (`pathBadge`) and path look (`pathLook`, `data-look`, which `paintIcon` reads). The guilds, Sniper lodge and Musket hall are drawn in `tower-art.ts` (`guild`: a hall over a dirt yard, with a fist, a stone, a crosshair or a burst on the roof), their troops in `battle-art.ts` (`drawGuildTroop`) and their shots in `projectile-art.ts` (`shotSprite`). Run `tests/specializations.test.ts`, `tests/knowledge-paths.test.ts`, `tests/tiles.test.ts` and `node tests/defend-upgrade.mjs`. Path looks are per-path structure sprites: `PATH_LOOKS`/`artLook` in `tower-art.ts` list the paths with their own art (every building path; Wall spikes' stakes keep the base art): the wizard tower's roof is a red hexagon on Pyromancy, an ice diamond on Rime and a lightning-seamed square on Stormcalling; Crusaders roof the barracks in lead under a white shield with a red cross; the Assassins' den's paths (`paintAssassinDen`, a soot-black hall with a dagger over a yard with a straw dummy) are a wine roof and bloodied yard (Bloodletters), a black roof with a hood and draped blades (Shadowcloak), a green roof with a vial and a poison pot (Venomblades) and a tarred roof with a skull and a bounty board (Headhunters); Rangers and Skirmishers re-roof the archer barracks and change its yard (a second target, straw dummies); Fire arrows bring an ember pot and scorched merlons, Sharpshooters a green canvas hide; Gun crews twin barrels, Siege shot a mortar; Spotters a spyglass and pennant, Signal fires a great beacon; Pyroclasm a bigger fire well under scorched tiles, Cinders smouldering coals under charcoal; Oil-soaked bait blackened crates and oil, Fortified crates iron bands. `tests/defend-damage-art.test.ts` checks every path has a distinct look, `structurePixels` and `paintStructureArt` take the look, and `DrawOptions.looks` (structure uid to path, from the battle's snapshot or the layout) reaches the city layer.

**The keep's tile and tree.** The keep is placed like a city tile: `moveKeep` onto open ground carries its tile (`carryTile`, shared with `moveCityTile`: gates, spikes and the corners it made go along), and onto another city tile still swaps; a press on the keep or its tile's open ground lifts `{ from: "keep" }`. Its upgrades live in `src/keep-upgrades.ts`, not `knowledge-paths.ts`: `KEEP_PATHS` (Bastions `BASTIONS`, Arrow loops `ARROW_LOOPS`, Boiling oil `BOILING_OIL`, City watch `CITY_WATCH`: the keep's guards' HP, then damage and reach, then twice as many trained twice as fast; Siege's `SIDE_GROUPS` leave it out, since castle guards never fight) are climbed side by side rather than chosen one of, ranks saved as `DefendSave.keepRanks` (optional, absent with none, `decodeKeepRanks`) and bought with `upgradeKeep`/`respecKeep`/`undoKeep` (free respec and undo, `keepAllocated`). `bonuses()` snapshots them as `Bonuses.keep`: `maxHpOf` multiplies the keep's HP and `Towers.stepKeep` shoots arrows and pours oil (`hurtEnemy` fire, `ignite`, steam effects); absent, nothing changes, so replays are untouched. Tapping or holding the keep while building opens its tree in the same `#defend-specialty` panel (`TreeTarget` `{ side: "keep" }`; `keepGraph`, `keepCardHtml`, `keepStep` in `ui/specialty.ts`, nodes `q:<branch>:<rank>`), and `inspect.ts` picks the keep with its arrow and oil reach. Run `tests/defend-keep.test.ts` and `node tests/defend-keep.mjs` (dev server required; isolated saves).

**Assassins' den.** A Melee Guild becomes an Assassins' den (`assassinDen`, `BECOMES`), training assassins (Soldier kind `"assassin"`, `ASSASSIN_UNIT`: quick and frail, chasing through `stepSwordsman` with `melee()`'s numbers, drawn by `drawGuildTroop` in black with a stabbing dagger). `defend/assassins.ts` owns their strikes (`assassinStrike`): every `every`th strike is critical (counted in `Soldier.strikes`, never rolled), and the den's four paths (`knowledge-paths.ts`, topic `assassinDen`): Bloodletters (`BLOODLETTERS`: bigger, more frequent criticals for less HP), Shadowcloak (`SHADOWCLOAK`: `Soldier.cloak`, 0 while cloaked; `nearestDefender` and `DefenderIndex` skip cloaked assassins, so no enemy picks them out, though blasts still hurt them; a strike unveils for `reveal` seconds, from the cloak hits `ambush` times as hard, at III a kill recloaks), Venomblades (`VENOM`: `Enemy.venom` multiplies all damage it takes in `hurtEnemy`, bites in `stepVenom`, and at III `spreadVenom` passes it on at death; `drawVenom` draws green drops) and Headhunters (`HEADHUNT`: the patrol's candidates sorted `toughestFirst`, a capped bounty of the target's max HP each strike, a wider leash at II and the whole city at III). `cloak`, `venom` stay absent without those paths, so replays are untouched. The Barracks' old Assassins path is retired: `refundRetiredAssassins` (on load, from the raw save) gives back its Knowledge (ranks worn standing or kept, pre-version-5 research, an unfinished project) and the barracks stand plain. Run `tests/defend-assassins.test.ts` and `node tests/defend-assassins.mjs` (dev server required; isolated saves).

**Spearmen line.** A Melee Guild becomes a Spearmen hall (`spearHall`, spearmen, `SPEARMAN`: a long `reach`), which becomes a Phalanx (`phalanx`, hoplites, `HOPLITE`: slow, taking `guard` of every blow through `hopliteGuard` in `hurtDefender`), a Javelin camp (`javelinCamp`, javelineers, `JAVELINEER`, through `stepArcher` throwing `Arrow.shot` `"javelin"`, drawn by `projectile-art.ts`'s `JAVELIN_ART`) or a Lancer stable (`lancerStable`, lancers, `LANCER`: fast riders whose first thrust at each enemy, `Soldier.lanced` remembering the last struck, is a `charge`). The Phalanx's Testudo, the Javelin camp's Barbed javelins and the Lancer stable's Winged lancers are the Spearmen hall's three branches, merging into the Valkyrie palace; the line's other paths are spares. Spearmen, hoplites and lancers chase through `stepSwordsman` with `melee()`'s numbers; `defend/spears.ts` holds their reach, speed, leash and strikes (`spearStrike`) and every path: Long spears (`LONG_SPEARS`, `runThrough` the enemy behind) and Set spears (`SET_SPEARS`: harder on the unhurt, staggering), Testudo (`TESTUDO`: comrades close by, `holdLine` slowing at III) and Spear wall (`SPEAR_WALL`: every enemy in reach), Barbed javelins (`BARBED`, `javelinOf`'s `Arrow.pin` and big game) and Throwing arms (`THROWING`, `javelinExtras`), Heavy lance (`HEAVY_LANCE`: harder charges, stagger, trample) and Winged lancers (speed, leash, healing charges). Their halls are `guild` sprites in `tower-art.ts` (`paintSpearHall`, `paintPhalanx`, `paintJavelinCamp`, `paintLancerStable`, a look per path) and their troops `drawGuildTroop`'s spears, round shield, raised javelin and horse (`horse`). `lanced` and the guard stay absent or exactly 1 without the line, so replays are untouched. Run `tests/defend-spears.test.ts`.

**Gunner line.** A Ranged Guild becomes a Gunner hall (`gunHall`, gunners with pistols, `GUNNER`), which becomes a Musket hall (`MUSKETEER`: slow, heavy single balls), a Machine-gun nest (`gunNest`, `MACHINE_GUNNER`: fast light bullets) or a Rocket battery (`rocketBattery`, `ROCKETEER`: very slow rockets, `Arrow.shot` `"rocket"`, bursting `blast` cells wide through `burstBall`, sparing your own). The Musket hall's Volley fire, the nest's Belt feed (`BELT_FEED`) and the Rocket battery's Cluster rockets are the Gunner hall's three branches, merging into the Gatling emplacement (`gatlingNest`, `GATLING_GUNNER`: an enormous rate of fire and a crawl). Each of the line's buildings has paths of its own, all read through `defend/gunners.ts` (`gunPaths`, `gunRange`, `gunDamage`, `gunReload`, `gunSpeed`, `gunHp`, `gunExtras`, `rocketOf`): the Gunner hall's Quickdraw (`QUICKDRAW`: reloads, a second pistol at III) and Duelists (`DUELISTS`: damage, range, every 3rd shot critical at III, counted in `Soldier.strikes`); the nest's Belt feed and Tripod (`TRIPOD`: range, damage); the Gatling's Gun shield (`GUN_SHIELD`: life, a quicker carriage from II), Overdrive (`OVERDRIVE`: all damage, no push, quicker at III) and Stopping power (`STOPPING`: all push, little damage; `gunShot` gives each bullet an `Arrow.knock`, and `knockBack`, from `stepArrows`, pushes its enemy straight back from the gun in short steps that stop at anything solid in `foeSolid`, fliers only at the board's edge, bosses, fortresses, boats and the unyielding not at all); the Rocket battery's Cluster rockets (`CLUSTER`: wider bursts, bomblets through `Arrow.grenade` from II, burning ground at III; `burstBall` leaves no blaze for a grenade whose `life` is 0) and Salvo racks (`SALVO`: reloads, two then three rockets a launch). All of them shoot through `troops.ts`'s `stepArcher` (`RANGED`, `SHOT`); their halls are `guild` sprites in `tower-art.ts` (`paintGunHall`, `paintGunNest`, `paintGatlingNest`, `paintRocketBattery`, each with a look per path), their guns in `battle-art.ts` (`drawGuildTroop`'s `GUN` table, the Gatling's turning barrels, the loaded rocket's tip). Save version 6 (`migrateGunLine`): every Musket hall and Sniper lodge bought straight from a Ranged Guild turns back into one, its Knowledge (the change and its path ranks) refunded and those standing taken up. Run `tests/defend-gunners.test.ts` and `node tests/defend-gunners.mjs` (dev server required; isolated saves).

**Sniper lodge and Barracks paths.** `defend/snipers.ts` owns the Sniper lodge's five paths (topic `sniperLodge`): Marksmen (`MARKSMEN`: `sniperRange` +3, `bigGame` aims at the toughest in reach from II, `bulletOf` counts shots in `Soldier.strikes` for a headshot every 3rd at III), Ricochet (`RICOCHET`: `Arrow.glance`, which `bulletHit`, called from `stepArrows`, sends on to the nearest enemy not yet struck) Suppressing fire (`SUPPRESS`: `Arrow.pin` chills, staggers from II; `sniperReload` at III), Deadeye (`DEADEYE`: much harder hits, further at III; clear sight is every gunner's rule now, see Gun sight) and Piercing rounds (`PIERCING`: `pierceOf` aims the bullet at the end of its reach with an `Arrow.pierce` (`target` -1), which `stepArrows` hands to `flyThrough`, striking each enemy within its width of the line once, `pierceHits`, at a share of the damage; further at III). Points are centre-relative offsets; movement retains them and inventory copies clear them. `decodeDefendSave` drops invalid optional values independently; page load, range edits, upgrades and native battle construction clamp valid points. `Bonuses.buildingOrders` is the immutable placement snapshot; `DefendSim.orders`, `rallies`, `crosshairs` and Minelayer `mineAims` are live per-home state. Defend changes write back immediately; Ambush retains orders in the run through moves, upgrades and waves, and resets them on a new run. Siege saves/orders are untouched.

Building acquisition/deployment, strike spell areas (except Meteor's explosion), rime and banner influence use inclusive axis-aligned squares with the old radius as half-width. `enemiesInSquare`/`nearestEnemyInSquare` preserve native grid order; mode comparisons are deterministic. Groups counts neighbours in local grid buckets. Crosshairs first filter eligible enemies inside their clipped two-cell half-width square; Closest measures from the crosshairs there, otherwise the home. **Deployment and rally points.** `defend/deployment.ts`: every troop with a home building has a deployment range round its middle, `DEPLOY_RANGE` by kind (brawlers and stone throwers 14 cells up to the Hero and the shade 30) times `Bonuses.deployRange` (the Smithy's Deployment range row, `deployRange`, 5% a rank to 30, under All troops); `TRAINS` (moved from `troops.ts`) says what each building trains, `deployRangeOf`/`deployCircle` give a building's. The sim's soldier loop runs `fence` after each step, taking back a step that went further out past the range (and the route with it); swordsmen's `Patrol` and every hunt and `seek` only go after enemies inside it, strolls pick from `strollStreets` (the streets inside it, cached per home) and troops answer the war banner or the rally flag only when it stands inside it (`bannerFor`; a double-tapped flag out of every troop's range says so). Spells' troops (`home` -1) and a cloudborne palace's valkyries have no range. `DefendSim.rallies` holds each troop building's rally point for the battle (`setRally`, not saved, not in the replay hash); troops with nothing to fight walk to it (`postPath`, the nearest open cell, ringing it like the banner) instead of strolling or heading home. In battle a tap selects a building; a second press on it (or on its flag or crosshair) dragged aims it (only the selected building: a press on any other pans or taps) (`DefendPage.aimBuilding`, a `BoardAim`; let go without dragging, it is a tap): a troop building's flag through `rallyAim` (back on the building clears it, pulled in to the range, snapped onto a standing watch tower within `TOWER_SNAP` of it in range for buildings whose troops shoot flat, `climbsTowers`), a Minelayer's crosshair through `mineAim`, both riding `BoardPointers.raise` above the pointer like a dragged building. `inspect.ts` draws a selected troop building's range (dashed pale blue) and its flag at the end of a dotted line, framing a snapped tower in blue; the Tiles sheet lists the range (`buildingSheet`). Run `tests/defend-deployment.test.ts` and `node tests/defend-rally-aim.mjs` (dev server required; isolated saves).

**Building levels.** `src/building-level.ts` (`buildingLevel`, `smithyRows`): a building's level is 1 plus every Smithy upgrade (Forge level or Training rank) filed in its own topic and the shared All troops or All towers topic it fights under (the keep, walls, gate, spikes and wall ballistas their own rows in City), a rough read of investment, not of damage. In Siege a tapped building shows it over its top edge (`SiegePage.placeLevel`, `.siege-level`, `siege.level`): the castle snapshot's levels and training for the defense's buildings, the player's own for theirs; houses have none.
