# Siege

Ranked Siege raids: native castles, raid battle, DPS estimates, calibration, mana, entry/matches, ranks, identities, sky, colours, flags, victory and troop orders.

Moved from `AGENTS.md`; keep it current with the code it describes.

`campaign/raid.ts` runs two native Defend sides on one native map. Native soldiers/buildings are exposed to the other side through targeting adapters; damage routes to their owning side's existing `hurtDefender`/`damageBuilding`. Collision, repairs, walls and gates synchronize between sides. Rendering combines real native effects/units; target adapters never draw as monsters. The upper castle has native streets, houses, walls, gates, keep, towers and troop buildings. Every owned infantry copy retains its worn upgrades in a hidden staging home beyond the bottom edge and trains/replenishes its native garrison through ScenarioRules.recruits. The homes occupy no city cells and cannot be drawn, inspected or targeted; crowded rosters share entry points without dropping copies. No invented finite reserve or separate campaign attack physics. Destroying the keep wins; SIEGE_SECONDS (120) expires the raid. campaign/siege-ui.ts owns Siege-only controls; campaign/host.ts holds the shared thin page host. Siege labels use i18n/siege.ts with English fallback. Raid building targets select one reachable face using the native flow field, cached by collision version and half-second simulation tick, so infantry can breach and flank without multiplying splash damage. Battle-adjacent powers/logarithms use deterministic ratioPow/binaryLog in exact.ts. Both modes omit redundant mode/help headings; Siege keeps ranked loss consequences in opponent details. Campaign saves store Ambush best stars without retaining battle layouts or changing inventory/Defend placements. Mode navigation pauses; reload restarts Ambush. `campaign/castles.ts`, `siege-rating.ts` and `siege-progress.ts` preserve seeded bot offers and idempotent rated results; recovered started raids count as losses, completed targets are unrated practice. The legacy castle strength prior now selects a native defense budget and requires fresh calibration; obsolete prototype win-rate measurements were removed. See `docs/design/SIEGE_MATCHMAKING.md`. `tests/siege-math.test.ts` guards deterministic helper precision and the battle source arithmetic; `tests/siege-targeting.test.ts` checks native breach/flank target faces. `tests/campaign-native.test.ts` compares actual specialized projectile payloads against Defend and exercises native wall destruction/mitigation; `tests/campaign.test.ts` and `siege-rating.test.ts` cover copies, saves and ratings. `node tests/campaign.mjs` (alias of `campaign-native.mjs`) checks the cleaned pages, real board mouse/touch placement and selling, native rendering/camera/armies at 1280 × 800 and 390 × 844 (screenshots in `test-results/`); `node tests/combat-tab.mjs` checks main-app navigation.

**Native Siege castles.** siege-v2 snapshots save MMR, seed, identity and the actual native blueprint, Armory levels, Smithy training, Skills and side trees. castle-generator.ts performs seeded personality-weighted purchases, native copy upgrades/merges, native quarter arrangement, bounded side ranks and native damage-training refinement; a legal ceiling witness supplies the high end. castle-decode.ts bounds layouts/catalog ranks before fitLayout; malformed optional ranks/portraits drop independently. Revision-one offers regenerate at their seed/MMR; interrupted legacy raids settle once as losses and completed results survive. battlefield.ts generates only the saved castle through fitLayout/generateCity, then appends hidden staging homes beyond the bottom edge (no city cells, art, inspection or targets). Native Barracks uses optional ScenarioRules.recruits for attack-owned hidden homes; its absent default preserves normal intact-building recruitment. A grass entry strip keeps native boulders clear of staging doors. CampaignSim and the HUD share SIEGE_SECONDS (120), and the Siege camera starts zoomed toward the castle/approach. RaidBattle takes the saved defense levels and native bonuses; there is no flat defense multiplier. siege-budget.ts interpolates generated division anchors from the native calibration curve. Regenerate with tools/siege-division-dps.ts. tests/siege-castles.test.ts pins 102 castle snapshots across divisions, native upgrade legality, budget tolerance, migration and table freshness. node tests/siege-native.mjs renders/recruits Bronze, Gold, Diamond and Grandmaster castles on desktop/phone.

**Siege damage estimates.** `defend/average-dps.ts` (`averageDps`) gives every building an average DPS from the battle's own constants: garrison (`soldierCap`) or one tower, times damage per hit (Forge levels, `Bonuses` training multipliers, the building's path rank), times hits per attack against a crowd of `CROWD.density` enemies a square cell (`inArea`, `alongLine`, `chained`, capped at `CROWD.most`), over the seconds between attacks; crits and first strikes count as their average, burning ground for `DWELL` seconds, the Shadow's compounding edge is averaged over a `FIGHT` and the Hawk tower's focus over `FOCUS_HP`. Not battle code, but only + − × ÷ so every engine agrees. A tweaked tower changes its estimate at once; nothing is measured or regenerated. campaign/castle-dps.ts sums it over a castle (`castleDps`), adding what watch towers' marks add (the strongest covering mark − 1, sampled at four fronts round each building), and prices buildings at a Forge band's reference city (`defenderDps`); it never drives battle damage. campaign/castle-model.ts defines native scratch castles on CASTLE_TILES; native-build.ts uses native purchases/copy upgrades/merges; siege-dps-catalog.ts enumerates every path/rank and five upgrade bands. tools/siege-dps-ceiling.ts (seconds) searches legal native placements and writes the floor and ceiling castles to siege-ceiling.ts, whose `DPS_MIN`/`DPS_MAX` are their live estimates. The ceiling is a bounded-search witness, not a global-optimum proof. See docs/design/SIEGE_DPS.md. tests/siege-dps.test.ts checks the estimate reads the catalog, grows with the Forge and path ranks (bar trade-off paths), shared-tree monotonicity and native legality; node tests/siege-dps.mjs renders floor/ceiling at desktop/phone sizes.

**Siege mana.** campaign/siege-mana.ts owns the transient experiment: SIEGE_MANA enabled/start 1/cap 8, one mana per defending Soldier's final death (including heroes/summons), and SPELL_MANA costs Frost/Necromancy 1, Meteor 2, Reinforcements 3. CampaignSim installs optional ScenarioRules.spellGate on the attacker only. Native casts consult canCast/spend instead of reading or starting cooldowns (SiegePage keeps Necromancy armed rather than spend mana where no grave is in reach); absent hooks preserve Defend/Ambush cooldowns and replay output. RaidBattle credits actual native post-mitigation deaths once through its HP adapters; Hero Legend revival, buildings, civilians, attacker losses and friendly fire earn nothing. Dead cached unit proxies remain until one native sweep runs graves/frost/nano effects, with separate per-side pending-death sets (natural summon expiry and civilians returning home create no combat death). SpellBar's optional UI gate supplies affordability/fill/cost pips/accessibility without reading cooldown clocks. ManaCounter draws a static outlined pixel crystal and updates changed text only; SiegePage uses the existing frame loop, owned spell filtering, native aiming and ready cues. No mana is persisted or shared with other economies. Flip SIEGE_MANA.enabled to restore Siege cooldowns. tests/siege-mana.test.ts covers costs/refusals, real proxy deaths, revival, native summons and death effects; node tests/siege-mana.mjs covers melee kills, affordability, spending, Reduce Motion and phone/desktop fit.

**Siege calibration.** campaign/siege-army.ts defines explicit offline reference progression and builds infantry-only armies through castle-generator.ts's native greedy walk. tools/tune-siege.ts searches actual native raid win/loss brackets, each budget decided by the majority of training seeds 1–3; unfillable budgets are capacity failures, never losses. tools/fit-siege-curve.ts fits monotone log anchors between the floor and ceiling estimates the training report recorded (`floor`, `ceiling`), then tools/siege-division-dps.ts emits the 35 runtime anchors. Diamond I holds its pre-ultimate budget until Master. tools/calibrate-siege.ts validates separate seeds at every reachable division, with resumable caches keyed by `calibrationHash` over Siege's own sources (`TUNING_SOURCES`/`VALIDATION_SOURCES` in tools/siege-calibration.ts: the generator, estimate, armies and raid, deliberately not the buildings' code, so a tweaked tower needs no recalibration; tests/siege-calibration.test.ts fails when either published report is stale against them); --smoke runs two seconds without reporting a win rate. tools/report-siege.ts publishes complete observations in docs/design/SIEGE_MATCHMAKING.md. Calibration armies are assumptions, not observed player progression or probability guarantees. tests/siege-calibration.test.ts checks native army composition, smooth budgets, smoke execution and reproducible curve inputs. Generated castles retry deterministic placements before a dense native packing fallback, and a band stops once its greedy walk stalls (`STALL`), so no offer waits on futile arrangement searches; regression seeds cover upgrade boundaries and crowded layouts. NPC guards retain native Defend leashes, while attackers rally to the castle keep; RaidBattle shows them a house only where it blocks their march (`stuck`, `HOUSE_REACH`), so they don't spend the raid on homes the builders rebuild. The reference armies (`ARMY_POLICY`) assume basic guilds below the 1000 starting MMR, attuned halls without paths from there, ranked paths from Diamond and ultimates from Grandmaster. The ceiling witness stands in at the top only when the greedy walk can't fill a budget.

**Siege entry and matches.** campaign/siege-entry.ts renders the rank/progress screen, Find Match card, native lazy castle preview and result/rank changes. SiegePage retains the native battle and owns entry/match/battle view state. findMatch rolls offered/finished targets without changing records and refuses a started raid; Attack commits after native start succeeds. Back is unrated, Practice never settles again, and recovery of a started raid queues persistence after main has initialized the Mine/Library host. Main-page navigation pauses; preview observers and reveal/result animations dispose when leaving. The battle has no buildings sidebar. campaign/siege-hud.ts (presentation only) hangs each side's flag from a T-shaped pole (`bannerPlate`, `Banners`: the stand and the flag composited per pixel with their own black outlines, the flag's rows swinging in a gentle travelling wave on the wall clock at 15 fps, still under Reduce Motion) beside a parchment name/rank plate, the opponent top left and the player bottom right, and `TroopBox` counts the player's living troops by kind bottom left (garrison-icons.ts's `paintTroopBadge`, `initials` of `TROOP_NAMES`, the count), rebuilt only when a count changes. Plates have pointer-events:none and dim under an aim/pointer; the troop box sits above the strike bar, and on phones so does the player's plate. UI shows one MMR and no opponent-source badge. previews.ts shares one native renderer and bounded lazy image cache between Ambush cards and square Siege castle crops. tests/siege-entry.test.ts covers preview commitment rules; node tests/siege-entry.mjs covers entry/reveal/Back/Attack, portraits/aim, waving banners, the troop box, rank changes, reload recovery and Reduce Motion on desktop/phone.

**Siege identities.** campaign/bot-names.ts generates bounded hash-seeded handles from curated fragments with pair/fragment guards. bot-identity.ts generates native flags with zero to four stamped layers, contrasting adjacent source colours and an opaque first stamp. New offers save owner.flag; legacy offers keep their original names and may omit it. decodeFlag in profile/model.ts is shared by profiles and optional opponent portraits; malformed optional flags are dropped without resetting Siege progress. Revision-1 identity RNG draws remain consumed separately so new identities do not change legacy castle geometry/budgets. tests/bot-identities.test.ts samples 100,000 names and 10,000 flags, plus decoding and geometry fingerprints; node tests/siege-identities.mjs renders native portraits on desktop/phone.

**Siege ranks.** campaign/siege-ranks.ts owns seven ranks and five 80-point divisions (35 tiers), with Grandmaster I at 2720 and an extended top band to 3000. New players at 1000 are Gold III; the existing 100 MMR minimum leaves Bronze V unreachable by ordinary losses. rankOf supplies inclusive bounds/progress and rankLabel uses the nine-language catalog fallback. ui/rank-emblems.ts paints procedural 24-pixel badges with division numerals at 3×, without image assets. tests/siege-ranks.test.ts checks every supported MMR, boundaries and distinct badge designs; node tests/siege-emblems.mjs renders the gallery at desktop/phone sizes.

**Siege sky.** The live game uses the shared play-time sky and fixed-phase settings above. As a standalone fallback, `campaign/siege-sky.ts` derives phase, night, tint and natural shadows from battle time left (day to sunset at 70, dusk at 45, full night for the final 20 seconds). Siege passes calm clear weather and optional `DrawOptions.sun/tint`; these are absent in other modes. Daylight leaves torches off, dusk enables native lighting, and the timer uses phase colors. The troop box is translucent black with a brass border. `tests/siege-sky.test.ts` and `node tests/siege-sky.mjs` cover boundaries, pause and desktop/phone frames.

**Siege side colors.** `campaign/siege-colors.ts` keys red/blue inks by original native soldier IDs. Optional `DrawOptions.sides` selects cached per-ink silhouette layers in `unit-outline.ts`; individual sprites reuse a small tint mask, while procedural rims, Shadow cloaks, swords, garrison badges and Simple squares share the same inks. Bodies and civilian/building art retain their palettes. `node tests/siege-outlines.mjs` checks desktop/phone galleries, touching units, merged/individual outlines and unchanged body pixels.

**Siege flags.** Optional `DrawOptions.keepFlag` resamples the owner profile onto native cloth without changing default banner pixels. `campaign/siege-flags.ts` owns eight seeded decorative poles near wall corners/gates and open sites. Native impact observations snap them once; their collision, targeting and RNG never enter the sim. Optional `DrawOptions.scenery` paints below trees. Run `tests/siege-flags.test.ts` and `node tests/siege-flags.mjs` for placement, impact isolation, custom cloth and desktop/phone standing/fallen galleries.

**Siege flag-bearers.** `defend/flag-bearers.ts` supplies three homeless, 1-HP non-combat street walkers at Siege start. Its private hash avoids the battle RNG. Raid proxies mark them `untargetable`; primary aim filters (including summons, Hawk and Wisp) skip them while splash, fire, chains and piercing retain them. Their own cannon fire cannot kill them, and native defense troop-loss stats exclude them. Death pays `manaFor` two mana once through the existing raid credit. `SiegeFlags` observes carried/dropped flags and the crystal. Run `tests/siege-bearers.test.ts`, `node tests/siege-bearers.mjs` and `node tests/siege-mana.mjs`. Calibration tracks the walker source; phase 12 reran training and 136 held-out raids.

**Siege victory.** `campaign/siege-victory.ts` schedules the keep, all walls/gates and a seeded third of standing structures by distance. The result settles immediately; a bounded presentation clock delays results until the wave ends or the player skips. `defend/collapse-view.ts` copies rubble/lighting masks through optional `DrawOptions.collapsed`; it never damages the sim. Siege keeps one stable view from battle through victory, reading live native arrays until the first collapse, so scenery history is preserved. `SiegeFlags` burns cloth from the bottom, then raises/unfurls the player face over poles, carried/dropped flags and the ruined keep. Pause freezes the sequence; Reduce Motion completes it instantly, including when switched on mid-sequence. Dust/embers/shake honor Effects Off. Default banner pixels and other modes remain unchanged; full night now retains native torch shadows when sunlight reaches zero. Run `tests/siege-victory.test.ts` and `node tests/siege-victory.mjs` (0.5/1.5/3-second frames, native-state isolation, pause, settlement, tap skip and Reduce Motion on desktop/phone).

**Siege troop orders.** `campaign/siege-squads.ts` groups hidden native homes into Melee, Ranged, Mounted and Magic, with separate Hero/ultimate groups. `siege-squad-bar.ts` arms one group, plants all its homes’ native rallies on a board tap, and clears them on a second press; its counts include living troops and its colors/order are stable. Spell and squad selection cancel each other. `deployment.bannerFor` shares one native walking field per current staging destination, so all families (including heroes) obey orders without new AI; clearing restores the keep assault. Optional renderer `markers` paints colored native rally cloth above trees. The hidden global goal flag stays out of the picture so the defender/captured keep face remains visible; group markers end with combat. The toolbar scrolls on phones, and board focus keeps Escape working after pan. No orders are saved. Run `tests/siege-squads.test.ts`, `node tests/siege-squads.mjs`, `node tests/siege-victory.mjs`, `node tests/siege-sky.mjs`, `node tests/siege-staging.mjs` and `npm run test:render-cache`.
