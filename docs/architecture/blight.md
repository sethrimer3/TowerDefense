# Blight (Dark One) enemies

Darklings, Blight worms, flying Blight, Blight fortresses and ships, the aura, packing/pressure and their performance work.

Moved from `AGENTS.md`; keep it current with the code it describes.

**Darkling migration.** `defend/catalog.ts` defines Mini/Darkling/Large/Major/Significant Darklings as solid black 1/2/3/4/5-art-pixel bodies (`ART` = 8 per cell), all `faction: "darkOne"` and `scriptedOnly`. Splits are Darkling → two minis, Large → two Darklings, Major → three Large, Significant → two Major; reserved slots are 1/3/7/22/45. All Dark One enemies use the shared portal contour, including portraits; pixel enemies use the batched black-body union (`mergePixelGaps`); named outline-free body exports support artist replacements. `waves.ts` authors absolute waves 1–40 only: waves 1–10 retain their opening rosters; `DARKLING_WAVES` contains the exact, evenly interleaved 11–19 rosters, including 3/4/5/6/7 Blight Darts on waves 15/16/17/18/19 and one Blight Prism on wave 19; wave 20 releases one 50-segment Blight Worm; `WORM_WAVES` interleaves Small/Medium Blight Worms on waves 21–39, and wave 40 releases three bosses. Authored difficulty sums initial costs and never draws randomness. Later procedural waves and Ambush exclude scripted kinds. `slots` multiplies a chain's full split tree by its segment count; `waveCap` remains 5000 except wave 20 (7000 for the complete 6800-body tree) and wave 40 (21000 for three complete boss trees, 20400 bodies), shared by release, auxiliary spawn and death splits. Do not raise the global cap.

`blight-worm-tuning.ts` owns the exported `BLIGHT_WORM` tuning (re-exported by `blight-worm.ts`): 5/8-cell spacing, 300 HP per segment, speed 3 surfaced/4 underground, 10–18-second surface spells, damage 4 on contact/1.25 per underground building pass (one quarter of the former 5), and a 4× head weak spot. `blight-worm.ts` owns deterministic bounded arc-length rings on optional `Enemy.worm` state. The head enters at the top edge with its tail unspooling outside; followers sample the head's actual trail and phase markers, including every bounce vertex. Cuts preserve the trail behind the new head and start independent phase timers. Surface heads ignore keep/flow/bait, wander with `sim.rand` (except on a Defend city's worn trails, where they slither down the trail instead: `followTrail`, see `defend-play.md`'s Worn trails) and probabilistically return toward city land when far away, stop at native structure contact (including `contact.ts`’s `fenceContactRects` for painted rails inside collision cells), and select clear ≥45° rotations from fixed sqrt-derived constants (reverse if trapped); every segment strikes buildings and runs over people with its own cooldown. Worms never pack or transfer Blight pressure, but still drain color. Freeze/stagger do not stop them; chill slows the head. Burrowed segments are fully immune (including piercing), untargetable, off spikes/health bars, drawn as lighter soft shadows with a heavy pixel-art dirt fountain at each head and sparse clods at every body segment, and pass through all terrain. Each dive uniformly chooses a random standing, non-hidden grounded building in stable ID order with one `sim.rand` draw and commits a straight ray through it. It stays under until it has reached that target and spent one uninterrupted second entirely outside placed structure footprints (including troop buildings, keep and reserved rubble tiles), then surfaces on dry open ground. `wormOnTowerTile` checks the whole segment/contour radius. Underground board edges bounce using the same fixed-angle rotation table with board-only clearance; terrain and structures never deflect it below ground. Every follower traverses the exact bounce vertex. A cut inherits its current target/reached marker and per-building pass ledger without rerolling or double-hitting. Each underground segment hits each encountered building once per pass. Underground deaths hatch three surfaced Significant Darklings on nearest open ground. One visible bar per worm kind sums that kind’s living segment HP. Dirt launch phases are presentation-only hashes of segment ID, clod and sim time, with 48 head clods versus four per body; Effects Off suppresses dirt and Reduce Motion freezes it while retaining the shadow. All worm headings, target/reached markers, continuous-clear timers, rings, phases, pass sets and cooldown maps are transient and absent on other kinds; no worm-only random draws occur without a worm.

`dark-drain.ts` retains fixed-bucket presentation-only density and reused 144 × 208 fields; saturation drain and night heat remain independent of movement/effects settings. Tests: `tests/defend-blight-worm.test.ts`, `tests/defend-darklings.test.ts`, portal/crowd/contact/wave-progress/replay tests, `node tests/blight-worm.mjs` (isolated 1280 × 800 and 390 × 844, portal joins, wall bounce, shadow, split, standalone Significant and 450-body frame timing), existing darkling/crowd/contact/contour/render-cache browser checks. Replay scenario `mixed` reaches wave 11 at 80 seconds: its golden is intentionally re-recorded for the new authored wave; city/fence/save goldens stay fixed. Run `npx -y node@24 --experimental-transform-types --import ./tests/pin-random.ts --test tests/defend-replay.test.ts` for the cross-engine check. Keep [enemy migration tracker](docs/design/ENEMY_MIGRATION.md) current as rosters change.

**Worm city return.** `worm-city-return.ts` measures distance to the nearest point on the actual city footprint, using cached occupied row spans per map/mask (fixed during a Defend run). It respects concave gaps and replaced masks; it never targets a keep/flow field or scans the whole board per head turn. `WORM_CITY_RETURN` in `blight-worm-tuning.ts` starts the return chance beyond 2.5 full eight-cell city tiles (20 cells), rises linearly and caps at 95% at five tiles (40 cells). At an ordinary surfaced head turn, a successful roll points toward that city edge and applies the existing small random turn for angular variety. All three sizes and heads created by cuts share it. The fixed draw order is wander angle, return roll only when beyond the margin, then turn interval; nearby heads retain their original two draws. Followers still sample the resulting exact trail. Underground straight rays, dive timers, contact and bounces retain their native rules. The cache allocates lazily only for worm turns and adds no saved or enemy state. Tests: `tests/defend-worm-city-return.test.ts` checks probability, concave geometry/translation/replaced masks, each size’s successful/failed/nearby/timed/underground cases, variable approaches, same-seed determinism and 36 starting-position/seed approaches within 40 seconds. Run existing worm/brood tests, replay (including Node 24), full suite/build and native worm browser checks. This is intended worm steering tuning; re-record a replay only if a fixture actually reaches it.

**Worm broods and short-piece deaths.** `blight-worm-tuning.ts` exports `SMALL_BLIGHT_WORM` (7 segments, 2 × 2 art pixels, spacing 2/8 cell, 60 HP, contact 1, burrow 1/4, each segment releases three Darklings) and `MEDIUM_BLIGHT_WORM` (14, 3 × 3, spacing 3/8, 180 HP, contact 2, burrow 1/2, three Large Darklings). Both share the boss's speed, fixed-table bounces, arc-length trails, building-targeted underground rays/edge bounces, immunity, 4× head damage, contact ledgers and status rules. Surface spells are four times as long (40–72 s versus 10–18 s), including new heads after cuts and later resurfacing. Per-kind entry offsets leave only the head on board while the tail unspools. Per-kind body geometry scales contact, clearance, burrow width, shadow and spray; bounded trails retain only that kind's length. `isBlightWorm` gates all family-specific spawn, targeting, damage, crowd, status and art paths; these states/RNG draws remain absent for other enemies. All sizes are scripted-only. Small/Medium entries belong to Amber Desert; the boss also appears there on wave 40. Artist bodies export outline-free at 2/3/5 pixels.

`cullShortWorms` detects living runs through the existing leader links, independent of array order: every piece with fewer than four segments dies, including a short original head piece and boss fragments. Four-segment pieces survive and gain their own head/timer/trail. Native `sweepAway` settles worm collapses and death-effect cuts before promotion, counting/hatching each death once (including underground pieces, whose young emerge on open ground). Collapsed pieces are not promoted and consume no head-initialization rolls; normal offspring spawn rolls still occur. The no-worm death path preserves its existing order. Waves 21–39 use the explicit tuning table in `WORM_WAVES`, with strictly increasing total split-tree HP, body reservations and initial cost; 1316–4760 total bodies fit the regular cap. Wave 40's three independent 50-segment bosses have 20400 total bodies and 269100 total HP; only that wave raises its cap to 21000, leaving wave 20 at 7000 and late procedural waves at 5000. Tests: `tests/defend-worm-broods.test.ts`, existing worm/darkling/crowd/contact/replay checks and `node tests/worm-broods.mjs` (desktop/phone joins, shadows/dirt, short-piece deaths, portraits, triple assembly and mixed-worm timings). All full split trees must spawn; verify the Node 24 replay without changing unrelated goldens.

**Flying Blight.** `blight-fliers.ts` defines the three-way release for Blight Dart, Blight Prism and Greater Blight Prism. All three are flying Dark One enemies and remain `scriptedOnly` so only authored placements can release them. Waves 15–19 add 3/4/5/6/7 darts respectively, with one prism on wave 19; Greater Blight Prism still awaits wave placement. Existing ground rosters are retained; split-inclusive body totals are 3353/4504/4415/4796/4951, all below the unchanged 5000 cap. Darts are black triangle/chevron paper-airplane silhouettes (6/8 cell, 10 HP, speed 2.4). The two equilateral prisms (12/8 and 20/8 cells, 90/270 HP, speeds .35/.22) each split into three darts, at exact 120-degree unit rays rotated onto the parent's heading with sqrt-derived constants. They scatter for .65 seconds through terrain before native flying AI resumes; only optional `Enemy.dartBurst` and flying-Blight facing are added. Native chill/freeze/stagger/impalement and per-wave caps apply; there are no extra random draws. `blight-flier-art.ts` draws simple three/four-vertex silhouettes through the existing shared portal contour; artist overrides use the exported pixel silhouette instead. Three/four nested inlaid triangles rotate at 1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8 of a six-second rotation, all returning together after 24/48 seconds. Their strokes stay within the body's incircle and bypass silhouette capture to avoid interior contour work. Reduce Motion holds the angles at zero; Simple enemies retains the silhouette/perimeter and omits the inlay. Shadows remain under the unit layer. `blight-flier-shapes.ts` supplies outline-free 6/12/20-pixel body sources via `basicUnitPieces`, with named PNG replacements. Tests: `tests/defend-blight-fliers.test.ts`, `node tests/blight-fliers.mjs` (desktop/phone gallery, animation/alignment, Reduce Motion, both graphics and Simple/merged settings, PNG replacement, native split and a 400-flier warm-render benchmark). The contact browser fixture checks the shared burning palette rather than the retired crimson rim. Wave placement is an intended gameplay change; replay fixtures must still match or be re-recorded only if they reach the changed waves.

**Blight walking fortresses.** `blightCannon`, `blightLance` and `blightFortress` are square black Dark One walkers, scripted-only pending authored wave placement. Small variants carry one cannon or laser; the medium carries both independently. Native assembly, collapse, turret-controlled lifetime, proportional leg slowdown, part reservations and one enemy kill credit apply. Optional `fortressPart.weapon` selects `blight-fortress.ts` weapons: energy-tagged native siege balls travel and splash through `hostileBurst`; lasers immediately pierce every person/structure intersecting their full range and leave short-lived `sim.blightLasers`. `blight-fortress-art.ts` shares the portal contour in both graphics and Simple settings, with black articulated limbs/mounts, orange cannonballs and deep-orange beams. Fixed 18/40-particle fields in `blight-fortress-particles.ts` turn orthogonally, fade tails to transparent and replace wall exits at random inward-facing edges using cosmetic hashes only. WeakMap fields are bounded and Reduce Motion uses a separate still field. The three black square body sources are exported/replaceable through `basic-unit-art.ts`; particles remain separate. Catalog/journal discovery decode automatically accepts the kinds. No existing wave, cap, save version or replay golden changes. Tests: `tests/defend-blight-fortress.test.ts`, `PLAYWRIGHT_EXECUTABLE=/usr/bin/chromium node tests/blight-fortresses.mjs` (Vite; desktop/phone, motion/settings, portraits, exports, weapons).

**Blight ships.** `catalog.ts` exports `BLIGHT_SHIPS`, mapping eight Blight kinds (Dinghy, Sailboat, Cutter, Cog, Skiff, Sloop, Galleon, Ark) to their native boats. Both catalog families derive from the same `BOAT_ENEMIES` definitions, preserving native insertion order for procedural rosters. Blight copies share every statistic and `BoatDef` setting, are `faction: "darkOne"` and scripted-only pending wave placement, and use black hull/deck materials, orange sails and red rails/runes/bow crystals in `boat-art.ts`. Native mast counts, cabins, geometry, sixteen headings, four frames, water, ramming, sinking, winter ice and carried lights are reused. Ship art now honors Reduce Motion; Simple Blight ships retain the recolored hull. All 512 Blight heading/frame sprites are exported as independent replaceable sources; journal entries and discovery decoding include the new kinds. No wave, save-version or replay-fixture changes. Tests: `tests/defend-blight-ships.test.ts` (native behavior/geometry parity, material colors, kills/discovery), `PLAYWRIGHT_EXECUTABLE=/usr/bin/chromium node tests/blight-ships.mjs` (Vite; desktop/phone comparison gallery, settings, motion, portraits and exports).

**Blight plasma fortress.** `blightPlasmaFortress` is a scripted-only large square Blight walker (4.5 cells, 12 legs, one centered `weapon: "plasma"` turret, 4000 aggregate turret HP). It shares native collapse, air-only rooftop targeting, grounded body damage, dragging and turret-controlled death. `blight-plasma.ts` owns `BLIGHT_PLASMA`: 15-second locked-aim charge, 0.75-body-diameter ball, range 14, three-second recovery, parabolic-shot lifetime, a 6.5-cell-radius six-second sphere with twelve half-second pulses, and 24-second dense scorches. `Enemy.plasmaCharge/plasmaFired` exist only while used; `sim.plasmaShots/plasmaSpheres` are transient arrays stepped after native siege shots. Enemy, troop, civilian and structure damage uses native gates regardless of allegiance, including Blight friendly fire; shields/guards/raised-chassis immunity and magic-water counterplay remain native. Area hits deduplicate grounded body/mounts. Impact takes only the remaining time in a step, with bounded pulse counts and epsilon-safe expiry; destroying the turret interrupts a charge, while released shots/fields persist. `blight-plasma-art.ts` draws the glowing projector, growing spherical orb, arc/shadow/trail and translucent pulsing sphere; Reduce Motion holds decorative orbit/pulse cycles and removes the trail, preserving combat telegraphs. Large bodies contain 72 cosmetic Tron particles. `Scorch.plasma` selects triple branching arms, longer cracks and more forks through the existing cached `blast-art.ts` source, with a distinct cache key; ordinary scorch seeds/shapes stay unchanged. The body source exports through the catalog and the journal/tester discover the new kind automatically. Wave placement remains pending; no existing rosters, replay fixtures or save versions change. Tests: `tests/defend-blight-plasma.test.ts`, `PLAYWRIGHT_EXECUTABLE=/usr/bin/chromium node tests/blight-plasma.mjs` (Vite; charge/arc/sphere/aftermath gallery, native crack-density comparison, desktop/phone, settings, portrait and body export).

**Enemy packing and Blight pressure.** `defend/enemy-crowd.ts` owns a per-sim live quarter-cell body index: stable bodies reserve their slots, axis sweeps clamp movement flush, and new/displaced bodies use deterministic shared placement cursors with a phase-wide probe budget. `DefendSim` brackets the native fixed-step phase; steering/ice slides, contact snaps, boats and cubes use the same gate. Airborne/underground units, attached parts and raid proxies are not independent ground bodies. Valid queued Blight skips repeated steering while cooldowns/abilities advance. Multi-source breadth-first passes propagate a front's current native target across touching living `darkOne` bodies, once per member/pass; each ready rear spends its own cooldown and basic damage through native defenses. Geometry is checked per source; death/displacement stops delivery. No random draws, recursive per-attacker chain walks, all-pairs collision checks, new saved fields or release-cap changes. Trapped/full arrivals wait outside the board within bounded placement work. `unit-bodies.ts` centralizes the existing TROOPS map (Simple re-exports it) and defender size; Blight closes to defender body/contour contact before biting, including a defender already inside the formation. `simple-units.ts` batches black Darkling fills; `UnitOutline.mergePixelGaps` marks their rectangle coverage for a clipped four-shift alpha intersection in `unit-contours.ts`, using one reused scratch canvas to fill narrow cracks black; three nested feather bands blend wider necks into fire as bodies separate. This is an intentional movement/damage change, so `defend-replay.golden.json` is re-recorded; city/fence/save fixtures stay fixed. See `docs/design/BLIGHT_PRESSURE.md`; tests: `tests/defend-crowd.test.ts`, `node tests/defend-crowd.mjs`, existing contact/contour/aura/cache checks, full suite/build.

**Dark One aura follow-up.** `dark-drain.ts` shares fixed-bucket counts between a grayscale field and a smaller-radius dim, deep orange night-heat field; both use the existing 144 × 208 storage, centroid patches and max blending. Ten neighbors start a visible 22% drain (previously ~2%); an easing rise reaches full grayscale with preserved tonal contrast near sixty. The halo uses `DARK_GLOW.rgb` `[212,58,6]` at maximum opacity 0.32. The old source-over gray contrast wash is removed; saturation blending alone preserves luminosity. A lone dark enemy has a faint night halo; strength/radius grow locally and opacity follows `DrawOptions.night`; when the shared world clock is active, `render.ts` gates this separately to `phase === "night"` (sunset/dusk/twilight/dawn stay glow-free). `DARK_UNIT_INK` remains deep crimson `#9c1235` for Siege side colors; Dark One enemies now use the fiery portal contour. `DarkDrain.prepare` runs before combat to choose protection; `render.ts` captures unit/projectile art in one reusable screen canvas only while needed, keeping ground effects outside it. Its alpha is removed from the effect mask after world scenery/rain are painted, preserving bodies/shots and canopy ordering; contours/UI still draw later. Actor capture shares combat-cache invalidation, and the screen mask resizes only with the viewport. No scene snapshots, readbacks, blur filters, per-enemy lights, gameplay changes or golden updates. Both cues persist with Effects Off/Reduce Motion. `tests/darkling-aura.mjs` checks actual environment desaturation, unit/shot preservation, night/day/group strength, desktop/phone DPR, paused cache reuse and 10,000-enemy bounds; existing Darkling, contour, contact and render-cache checks also apply.

Aura blends are clipped to the fields’ affected world bounds (including interpolation margins); a small crowd does not require a full-viewport blend. Bounds and screen protection follow the same camera transform.

**Blight vegetation trails.** `defend/blight-trail.ts` records actual living ground Dark One movement in a bounded half-cell exposure field on `DefendSim.blight`, independent of the renderer. Travel distance and native body area (or bounded HP-derived strength for other Blight) accumulate; waiting, flying, underground travel, raid proxies and teleports do not paint trails. Grass dies first; bushes and trees with larger root/canopy radii require more accumulated traffic. `blight-art.ts` paints native-resolution dry turf and caches brown, thinning and leafless branch sprites from the existing sources; forest shadows shrink with the crowns. `render.ts`, `park-trees.ts`, `forest-art.ts` and both grass painters share this run-local state. Death persists through waves, resets with a new run, and remains visible with Effects Off or Reduce Motion. No collision, combat, RNG, saves or replay hashes change. Tests: `tests/defend-blight-trail.test.ts`, `node tests/blight-trails.mjs` (Vite required; isolated desktop/phone contexts, screenshots in `test-results/`).

**Blight performance.** Worm and ordinary chain followers use `DefendSim.livingEnemy`; the enemy phase builds its existing transient ID map whenever a leader link or fortress part is present, keeps live HP checks and clears it in `finally`. `unit-contours.ts` tracks conservative transformed sprite/vector/stroke bounds for the shared portal, with a full-canvas fallback only for opaque external `Path2D`/unknown geometry. Fire uses a separate reusable local surface with chunked growth/shrink, so destination-in cannot blend the full viewport for a small contour. Axis-aligned offscreen rectangles are skipped; overlapping intervals in identical pixel rows merge independently in each feather band before Canvas rasterization. Geometry stays fractional and fire remains screen anchored. `tests/blight-performance.mjs` compares native phases and rendering with reference strategies, asserts raster-work bounds and pixel parity, and reports timings without CI speed thresholds. `tests/blight-contours.mjs` compares cropped/full portal pixels (exact alpha; up to two RGB levels of Canvas premultiplication rounding at faint curve edges; normal/torch masks, every Blight species, transformed sprites, curves, strokes and offscreen bodies); worm tests check indexed/linear motion/cut/RNG equivalence and zero per-follower array scans. See `docs/BLIGHT_PERFORMANCE.md`; full suite/build and existing crowd/aura/contour/cache browser checks apply. No combat, cap, save or golden changes.
