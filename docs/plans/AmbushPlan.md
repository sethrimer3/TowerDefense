# Ambush plan

Ambush is the road-defence mode: enemies march a fixed track toward a keep, and the player builds towers beside it with gold earned in the level. This plan turns Seth's 2026-10-27 brief into ordered work for an AI agent. Read it top to bottom before starting; each phase is meant to ship as its own PR.

Related plans: [SiegePlan.md](SiegePlan.md), [DefendPlan.md](DefendPlan.md), and [OtherPlans.md](OtherPlans.md), which holds the work shared by every mode (fences as walls, the strike spell quick bar, tab icons, the currency bar, ropes).

## Reading of the brief

"Remove the 'how to play' and Ambush": this means the campaign page's header text, the **"How to play"** `<details>` and the big **"Ambush"** `<h1>` title above the board (`campaign/ui.ts` line 45), not the Ambush mode itself. ## Target design

### Level select

- When no level is running, the Ambush page shows a **level select** instead of the board: three columns, one row per zone. Each zone (`AREAS` in `src/defend/areas.ts`: Mossbound Ruins, Amber Desert, Ember Forge, Drowned Temple, Fungal Hollow, Frozen Vault, Violet Geode, Obsidian Crypt, Astral Sanctuary, Nadir) has **three levels**, so 30 levels in all. Columns are Level I, II, III; rows are zones, tinted with `areaStyle`.
- Each card shows a **small preview** of the level (the generated map, road, outposts and keep), its name, and its best star rating (0 to 3 stars). Locked levels are dimmed.
- Unlocking (default): clearing a level with any stars unlocks the next level in its zone; clearing Level III of a zone unlocks Level I of the next zone. Mossbound Ruins I is open from the start.

### Waves and enemies

- Every level has **20 waves**, ramping up. Enemies come only from the level's zone (`AREA_ENEMIES[area]` in `src/defend/area-enemies.ts`; Nadir uses every non-hatched species, as in Defend).
- Build each wave with `buildDifficultyWave(budget, rand, AREA_ENEMIES[area])` from `src/defend/waves.ts`, not `buildWave`, which ignores zones. Budget: a curve per (zone, level, wave), e.g. `waveDifficulty(1 + zoneIndex * 20 + (level) * 3 + wave)` scaled down for a road with no walls. Put the curve in one table (`AMBUSH_WAVES`) so it can be tuned; keep offspring/multipart reservations as `buildDifficultyWave` already does.
- Bosses: wave 10 and 20 of each level get the zone's toughest kind in the budget (optional, phase 7).

### The board

- The track starts **at the board's edge** and an **arrow** is drawn there pointing in, so the player sees where enemies come from. The arrow is board art (pixel art, black outline, gently pulsing; still under Reduce Motion), drawn in the overlay pass, not part of the cached city layer.
- Along the track stand **three settlements**: **Outpost 1** about a third of the way along, **Outpost 2** about two thirds along, and the **keep** at the end.
- **Outposts** are small generated towns (houses, streets, parks, exactly the seamless look of the screenshot) with a **fence round them** that also **crosses the track**. Enemies must break the fence where it crosses, and once inside they **destroy every building of the outpost before moving on** down the track.
- The **keep** sits at the end of the track inside a **castle wall** (Defend's wall stones) with its surrounding houses. Nothing can be built on that wall or inside it.
- **Flying enemies** ignore the track and the outposts and fly in a straight line from the level's entry point to the keep (Seth, 2026-10-27). They are the main threat to the keep itself, so anti-air near the keep matters.
- **Flier warning**: when the next wave contains fliers, show a warning before it starts (during the break, or as soon as the previous wave's spawning ends):
  - a **pixel-art red dotted line** from the level's opening (the track's edge entry) straight to the keep, its dots marching toward the keep;
  - a **red circle round the keep** that expands outward and fades, repeating;
  - a **red exclamation mark** popping in at the start of the line, at the entry point.
  All of it is board art with black-outlined pixels, drawn in the overlay pass (not the cached city layer), stays up until the fliers have spawned, and under Reduce Motion is drawn still (no marching dots, a single static ring). Put it in `campaign/flier-warning.ts`; the wave table says whether a wave has fliers (`ENEMIES[kind].flying`).
- Everything else is a free **build grid**, like Defend's quarter-tile grid. Buildings cannot go on **water**, the **track**, **rocks/boulders**, the **keep**, its **castle wall or castle area**. They **can** go over **outpost houses**: the outpost re-plans its houses round the new building (see Outposts below).

### HUD

- The build menu (left side panel, `defend-side`) has a **fixed header** that never scrolls away:
  1. On top, a **health bar** in the style of Defend's keep health (`keep-bricks.ts`) with a number, labelled **"Outpost 1"** while Outpost 1 stands, then **"Outpost 2"**, then **"Keep"**.
  2. Under it, the **gold** box (coin icon and count).
- Below the fixed header: the buildable towers (the palette), or, while a placed building is selected, its **building panel**.
- Wave controls: a **Send next wave** button and an **Auto-send** toggle. Sending a wave early **gives no bonus** (no gold, no time). Show the wave as `Wave 7/20` with the enemies left, like Defend's wave box.
- Strike spells: translucent quick-bar buttons in the board's bottom left (shared with Defend and Siege, see OtherPlans.md).

### Stars

- A level is won when all 20 waves are cleared and the keep still stands. If the keep falls the level is over at once: zero stars and no completion, even if outposts are still standing. Stars (confirmed by Seth):
  - keep standing, both outposts standing: **3/3**;
  - keep standing, one outpost standing: **2/3**;
  - keep standing, no outposts: **1/3**;
  - keep destroyed: **defeat**, 0 stars, nothing saved but the attempt.
- When Outpost 1 falls, a star visibly cracks off in the HUD, the label moves on to "Outpost 2", and enemies carry on down the track.
- The end screen shows the stars (with a small flourish and sound), kills, gold earned, and Retry / Next level / Level select.
- Save each level's best stars and show them on its level select card.
- Stars are also the currency of the Ambush star tree (below).

### Buildings and upgrades

- Ambush buildings are **base buildings with no Knowledge and no Smithy upgrades**: the battle is built with `NO_BONUSES` and default (zero) Armory levels, whatever the player has researched in Defend. The only lasting power comes from Ambush's own **star tree** (below).
- Strike spells the player owns are usable, cast at **base strength** (no Study paths, no Smithy rows). Confirmed by Seth.
- The palette sells exactly **Melee Guild, Ranged Guild, Archer Tower, Cannon Tower, Wizard Tower**, available to every player (not gated on Defend ownership).
- Tapping a placed building turns the build menu into its **building panel**:
  - a **portrait** of the building (the palette icon, drawn larger, in its current look);
  - stats: **kills**, **total damage**, **average DPS this wave**;
  - **upgrade options**: every next step in its Knowledge tree (`nextSteps` in `src/specializations.ts`: attunement, branch attunement, the 3 ranks), each with a **gold** price; buying one changes the building in place, exactly as Defend's tree does, but paid in Ambush gold and only for this level;
  - at the very bottom, **Sell** with the refund shown: **70% of all gold put into the building** (purchase plus every upgrade), rounded down to tens. **Exception (Seth, 2026-10-27): selling before wave 2 starts (in the build phase or during wave 1) refunds everything put in.**
  - tapping the board elsewhere or a close button returns to the palette.

### The star tree (Ambush's own upgrade tree)

Seth asked (2026-10-27) for Ambush to have its own upgrade tree, paid for in **stars**.

- **Currency.** The star pool is the sum of each level's best stars (30 levels × 3 = 90 at most), minus stars spent. Improving a level's best from 1 to 3 stars adds 2 to the pool. Stars are never lost by losing a level.
- **Separate from Defend.** The tree affects Ambush only. It never reads or changes Knowledge, Smithy levels, metals or Defend's trees, and nothing in Defend reads it.
- **Shape.** It follows the game's tree look (reuse `UpgradeGraph` with `TreeMotes` and the magic circle, like the keep's side-by-side tree in `src/keep-upgrades.ts`/`src/side-ranks.ts`). Suggested branches, climbed side by side, each 3 to 5 ranks with rising star prices (placeholder numbers, tune in balance):
  - **War chest**: more starting gold (+50 gold a rank).
  - **Bounty**: more gold per kill (+5% a rank).
  - **Masons**: the five base buildings cost less (−5% a rank).
  - **Quartermaster**: better refunds when selling (70% → 75/80/85%).
  - **Drillmasters**: base troops and towers hit harder (+5% damage a rank) and troops have more HP. These multiply Ambush's base stats; they do not unlock Knowledge paths.
  - **Palisades**: outpost fences and houses take longer to break (+15% HP a rank); rank III rebuilds the wooden fences and gates in stone.
  - **Keep walls**: the keep and its castle wall have more HP.
  - **Battle magic**: shorter strike spell cooldowns in Ambush (−10% a rank).
  - **Head start**: a free base building placed at the start of every level (rank I one, rank II two).
- **Respec.** Free and instant, giving every star back, like the keep's tree. Undo on any owned rank takes it and those above it.
- **Where it lives.** A **Star tree** button on the level select (the page with the stars), showing the pool. It is closed during a level.
- **Code.** `src/ambush-upgrades.ts` (pure: `AMBUSH_PATHS`, prices, `ambushBonuses(ranks)` returning plain multipliers), ranks saved as `CampaignSave.ambush.ranks` (optional, absent with none, decoded with `decodeSideRanks`). `CampaignSim` reads `ambushBonuses` when building the battle: gold and prices in `economy.ts`, damage/HP through a `Bonuses` built from `NO_BONUSES` with only those fields set, outpost and keep HP in `outposts.ts`, spell cooldowns through the sim's existing cooldown fields.
- **Tests.** Pool maths (best stars only, spending, respec), each branch's effect in a Node battle, save round trip, a browser check of buying and respeccing at both widths.

## Architecture guidelines

These matter more than any single feature. Follow them throughout.

1. **Combat stays Defend's.** Ambush adds geography, lifecycle and economy; towers, troops, projectiles, enemies and art run through `DefendSim`/`DefendRenderer` unchanged. New behaviour goes in `ScenarioRules` hooks or optional sim fields that are absent in Defend, so `tests/defend-replay.test.ts` and the other goldens stay byte-identical. If a golden changes in an Ambush PR, something leaked into Defend: find it rather than re-recording.
2. **Exact arithmetic in the battle.** Battle code may use only `+ - * /` and `Math.sqrt` with `intPow`, `sq`, `dist` from `src/exact.ts`. Today's `CampaignSim.followRoad` and `ambushField` use `Math.hypot`; replace them in phase 1. Drawing code may use `Math` freely.
3. **Deterministic levels.** A level's map, outposts, waves and seeds come from (zone, level index) only, through `hash01` and seeded streams; no `Math.random`. The same level always looks the same and its previews match.
4. **Pure modules, thin UI.** Keep level geometry (`campaign/levels.ts`), placement rules (`campaign/ambush-grid.ts`), outposts (`campaign/outposts.ts`), wave tables (`campaign/ambush-waves.ts`) and the economy (`campaign/economy.ts`) pure and unit-tested in Node. `campaign/ui.ts` only renders and forwards input. Consider splitting the Ambush page out of `CampaignPage` (`campaign/ambush-ui.ts`) since Siege and Ambush now share little UI.
5. **Reuse Defend's input and art.** Use `BoardPointers` for drags (ghost icon, raise-above-finger, magnifier, tap vs. pan), `edit-overlay.ts`'s quarter grid and footprint drawing for placement feedback, `paintIcon` for palette and portrait, `UpgradeGraph`/`ui/specialty.ts` for the tree if a full tree view is wanted. Do not hand-roll a second drag system like today's `dragPalette`.
6. **Saves.** Change `CampaignSave.ambush` to `{ stars: Record<levelId, 0..3>, unlocked: levelId[] }` (or derive unlocking from stars). Add it to `defaults()`, the `Save` type and `decode()` in `src/save.ts` per AGENTS.md. Old saves: drop the pad layouts (they no longer mean anything) and map `cleared: n` to 1 star on the first n Mossbound levels, so nobody loses unlocks. Battles are not saved; reload restarts the level, as now.
7. **Styling.** All new UI uses `theme.css` tokens (`--wood`, `--stone`, `--parchment`, `--brass-nails`, `--ink`) and `var(--font)`. The level select is parchment cards on wood, not a plain grid of buttons. Pixel art has crisp black outlines.
8. **Localisation.** New labels ("Outpost 1", "Keep", "Send next wave", "Auto-send", "Level select", star texts) go through `t()` with English entries in `src/i18n/` and `data-i18n` on text-only labels. Longer descriptions may stay English, as elsewhere.
9. **Mobile first.** Every screen must work at 390 × 844 and 1280 × 800. The side panel's fixed header must not push the board off a phone screen; on phones the build menu is the slide-in palette Defend already uses.
10. **Docs as you go.** Each PR updates AGENTS.md's Combat tab and campaigns paragraph, `CONTEXT.md` (new terms: Outpost, Star rating, Level select, Track) and `docs/design/AMBUSH_ECONOMY.md` when prices change. Tick this plan's phases as they land.
11. **Test every phase** in Node (pure modules, sim behaviour, saves) and with one browser script at desktop and phone width (`node tests/ambush-*.mjs`, isolated saves, screenshots in `test-results/`). Run `npm test` and `npm run build` before each commit.

### Phase 4: Outposts and the keep's castle

- [x] `campaign/outposts.ts`: for each outpost anchor on the track, an area (rounded rectangle, roughly 2 × 2 Defend tiles) straddling the track. Generate its houses, streets and parks with `generateCity` restricted to that area (city mask), seeded per level and outpost. The track runs through as road.
- [x] **Fence**: the outpost's outline is a fence (see OtherPlans.md: fences become wall-like buildings with ~10% of a wall stone's HP). Where it crosses the track it blocks the road; enemies must break it to enter. (2026-10-28, Seth: the road now meets a wooden gate there, or a stone gatehouse once Palisades III walls the outposts in stone, and the keep's wall has a gate where the road meets it; units come and go only by the gates.) Build this phase on top of the shared fence work, or, if that has not landed, give Ambush's outpost fences their own solid fence cells with the same HP rule and fold them together later.
- [x] **Keep castle**: the keep plus its houses inside a ring of Defend wall stones (`wall` mask in `generateCity`), the track ending at a gate or a breach point in the wall. Its cells are all in `blocked`.
- [x] **Re-plan on build**: when the player places a building over an outpost, regenerate that outpost's houses with the player's building passed to `generateCity` as a structure, so houses rearrange round it (deterministic for the same layout). Record the outpost's **initial** house count at level start; covered houses simply no longer exist.
- [x] **Outpost HP**: `breached ? standing / initial : 1`, where `breached` latches true the first time any enemy (or its body cell) is inside the outpost's area. So a player who covers every house still shows 100% until an enemy walks in, then it drops to 0 and the outpost falls at once, as Seth described. `standing` counts houses with HP > 0.
- [x] **Enemy behaviour** (`routeEnemy` hook): an enemy following the track that reaches a live outpost leaves the track and goes for the nearest standing outpost building (Defend's smash behaviour: attack the building it is next to, use the flow field to reach it); once the outpost has fallen, it returns to the nearest track point and carries on. At the keep, enemies attack the keep (and whatever blocks them) as in Defend. **Fliers** ignore the track, the fences and the outposts entirely and fly straight from the entry point to the keep (see Flying enemies). Keep all of this in `campaign/`, calling public sim helpers; do not branch Defend's enemy code on mode.
- [x] **Fliers**: a flier skips `followRoad`'s track and outposts and moves straight at the keep from wherever it spawned (the entry point), attacking the keep (or anything in its way, as Defend's fliers do) on arrival. Build the flier warning (`campaign/flier-warning.ts`, see The board) in this phase, with a Node test that flags exactly the waves containing fliers and a browser screenshot of the line, ring and exclamation mark at both widths.
- [x] Remove `lives`; leaking past the keep is impossible because the track ends there.
- [x] Tests: outpost geometry stays on the board and straddles the track; HP formula including the "covered everything" case; enemies raze then move on; keep falls → defeat; outposts never touch the keep's castle.

## Decisions and open questions

Seth confirmed on 2026-10-27:

- The removed items are the page's "How to play" box and big "Ambush" title, not the mode; the same goes for Siege.
- The keep falling is a defeat; stars are 1/2/3 by outposts surviving with the keep, shown on the level select.
- The Watch tower is not buildable in Ambush.
- Strike spells cast at base strength in Ambush.
- Ambush gets its own star-funded upgrade tree (see The star tree).

- Selling before wave 2 starts (build phase or wave 1) gives a full refund; after that, 70%.
- Ambush keeps Defend's one-quarter clear ring round each building.
- Flying enemies ignore the track and outposts and fly straight for the keep; a keep loss ends the level with zero stars even if outposts stand. A red dotted line, expanding red ring and exclamation mark warn of fliers in the next wave.

Implemented choices:

- All nine suggested star-tree branches are available. Prices are 2/3/5 stars for ranks I/II/III; Head start has two ranks at 3/7 stars. Free undo/respec conserves the 90-star maximum. See [star tree rules](../design/AMBUSH_STAR_TREE.md).
- Phases 0–8 are complete. The final scripted checks cover all 30 levels, a three-building Mossbound I clear, an 85-star Nadir III clear and 3× phone-viewport performance. See [balance results](../design/AMBUSH_BALANCE.md) and [performance results](../design/AMBUSH_PERFORMANCE.md). Balance estimates remain adjustable; the checked-in policy deliberately reports defeats and capped runs.
