Tower Defense Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

What the game keeps

Your progress, settings, player name and banner are saved only on your own device (in the browser's or the app's local storage). The game has no accounts and no servers of its own, and the developer does not receive this data.

Ads in the Android app

The Android app can show optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, app interactions and diagnostic data. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change that choice from the Shop's Privacy options. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases

Buying the game in the Android app goes through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the game only learns whether you own it. See Google's Privacy Policy.

The web version

The version on GitHub Pages shows no real ads and takes no payments. GitHub may log visits as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised on the game's issue tracker.
